# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

GoodWatch on the web (goodwatch.app). Inherits the shared product record in `../PRODUCT.md`. This file adds only what is specific to the web app.

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: one person deciding what to watch next, on a phone or a desktop browser. Secondary: members who score, log, and import their history to sharpen their taste profile. Visitors arriving from search engines and shared links land on title, person, and list pages without an account.

## Product Purpose

Help a person go from "nothing to watch" to a confident choice, and give members a place to curate the taste data that makes those choices better.

## Operating Context

- Main areas: Discover (search, mood, filters), For You recommendations, title pages (score, availability, taste fit, episodes), the member's library and taste, and imports from other trackers and streaming services.
- Many visitors arrive on a single title or person page from a search engine or a shared link, so those pages must stand on their own.
- Shared pages and lists produce Open Graph images for social posts.
- `/use-with-ai` explains how to use GoodWatch from AI apps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Mobile web is a first-class target, not a scaled-down desktop.
- Member-visible features ship to everyone. There are no per-member feature switches.
