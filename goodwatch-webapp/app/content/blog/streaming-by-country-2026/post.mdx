## Studio Ghibli, almost everywhere but Japan

{f.ghibli} of the {f.ghibli + data.lists.ghibli_apart.length} Studio Ghibli films in the list are on Netflix in {f.ghibli_netflix_countries} countries: every country in our data where Netflix is tracked, except the United States and Japan. In the US, they're on HBO Max. In Japan, where they were made, none of the {f.ghibli} streams at all: the data lists no offer of any kind there, not even to rent or buy, and JustWatch's Japanese site says the same.

The films: <TitleRun items={data.lists.ghibli} />.

The exception is <TitleLink item={data.lists.ghibli_apart[0]} /> ({data.lists.ghibli_apart[0].year}), whose rights sit apart from the others with the publisher Shinchosha. Netflix has streamed it in Japan since July 2025, and it's on Netflix in all {data.lists.ghibli_apart[0].netflix} of our Netflix countries.

It goes beyond Ghibli. Of the {f.japanese} films in the list that were made in Japan, {f.japanese_in_us} stream on a subscription in the US and {f.japanese_in_japan} in Japan.

## How we counted

- **The titles.** Every movie and show in GoodWatch's catalogue with at least 50,000 IMDb votes, a GoodWatch score and a poster, first released at least 180 days before {day}: {fmt(M)} movies and {fmt(S)} shows. Shows leave out talk, news, soap and reality shows. The vote count keeps out mismatched entries that other vote counts let in. The best-rated cut is the {fmt(data.movies.top_cut)} movies and {fmt(data.shows.top_cut)} shows with the highest GoodWatch score.
- **The data.** TMDB's watch providers, which come from JustWatch, read for every title on {day}. Catalogues change every week, so every number here is that day's.
- **On a subscription.** A service lists the title in that country as included in its subscription. A service's plans and add-on channels count once: Netflix with ads is Netflix, and HBO Max through Prime Video is HBO Max.
- **Pay-TV apps count.** Services that come with a TV or internet plan, such as Movistar TV in Chile and Colombia or Lepsi TV in Czechia, count as subscriptions. They're often the biggest service in their country.
- **Not counted.** Bloodstream, Artify, Artiflix and Cultpix list a few public-domain and classic films in about 100 to 138 countries. They'd make those films look available everywhere, so they're left out.
- **Free.** Free with ads (such as Tubi, Pluto TV or Plex) and free with a library card (such as Kanopy and Hoopla) are counted apart from subscriptions.
- **Countries.** The charts show 56 larger markets. The table and the files have all {data.countries.length}. Very small territories often show a neighbor's catalogue.
- **Limits.** JustWatch tracks most streaming services, but not every local one, and not every title of the ones it tracks. A low count can partly mean that a country's own services aren't tracked, as in Israel. "{f.ghibli_netflix_countries} countries" for Netflix means the countries in our data where Netflix is tracked; Netflix itself operates in more than 190.
- **Checked by hand.** Before publishing we checked the main findings on the services' own sites: the Ghibli films on JustWatch Japan and U-NEXT, 35 titles on U-NEXT's title pages, Movistar TV Chile's public catalogue, and which services JustWatch tracks in Israel.
