Netflix knows which household you're in. Your friend who texts "it's on Netflix" does not.

## TL;DR

**A service name without a country is a rumour.**

- **Ghibli:** 17 Studio Ghibli films are on Netflix in 129 countries and have no offer of any kind in Japan. They were reported leaving Netflix on August 1. They didn't.
- **Movies:** The US streams 3,478 of 4,683. Netflix US has 548 of them, 50th of 55. March's price rise took the Standard plan to $19.99; the rank came at no extra charge.
- **One service:** Japan's U‑NEXT carries 1,863 of the movies by itself. Prime Video, the biggest in the US, has 639. Japan can't stream Totoro and can stream nearly everything else.
- **Shows:** Netflix carries the most of the 717 shows in 55 of 56 larger markets. The friend who only watches shows was right all along. Don't tell them.

## Japan would like its movies back

*[Spirited Away](/movie/129-spirited-away)*, *[My Neighbor Totoro](/movie/8392-my-neighbor-totoro)*, *[Princess Mononoke](/movie/128-princess-mononoke)* and 14 more are on Netflix everywhere our data tracks it, except the US and Japan.[^129] In the US they're on HBO Max, which was Max until July 2025 and has belonged to Paramount since October 6. The films haven't moved; their landlord has changed names twice and owners once.

In Japan the data lists nothing, except *[Grave of the Fireflies](/movie/12477-grave-of-the-fireflies)*, whose rights sit apart with the publisher Shinchosha; Netflix first streamed it there in July 2025. So the one Ghibli film Japan can stream is the one about two children starving. Nobody planned that.

Ghibli's producer said in September that people got bored of the AI Ghibli images "as expected." The real ones are harder to shift: Spanish outlets reported them leaving Netflix on August 1, and on October 18 all 17 were still there.

## Before you buy another subscription

In the US, 1,575 of these movies stream free with ads and 1,010 come with a library card through Kanopy or Hoopla. The library is the last streaming service that doesn't ask who's in your household. LA County cut Hoopla borrows from eight a month to five in January, so even the library is tightening the plan.

Recommend the movie. Say the country. If it's Ghibli, check where your friend lives before you ruin their evening.

## Methodology and credits

Snapshot of October 18, 2026. Every movie and show in GoodWatch's catalogue with at least 50,000 IMDb votes, a GoodWatch score and a poster, released at least 180 days earlier: 4,683 movies and 717 shows. Availability from TMDB's watch providers, which come from JustWatch, read for every title that day in all 139 countries covered. Subscription offers count once per title and country; a service's plans and channels are grouped. Bloodstream, Artify, Artiflix and Cultpix are left out. Charts show the 56 larger markets. A missing listing isn't proof a film is unavailable. Main findings were hand-checked on JustWatch Japan, U‑NEXT, Movistar TV Chile and the Israel provider list on October 19, 2026.

[^129]: 129 means the countries in our data where JustWatch tracks Netflix, minus the US and Japan. Netflix itself operates in more than 190. Its 2020 licence excluded the US, Canada and Japan; Canada was added later.

[^israel]: The list is picked by IMDb votes, so it leans American. Israel's 725 is partly a data gap: JustWatch doesn't track Disney+ there, or the pay-TV services yes, HOT, Cellcom TV and Partner TV.

[^unext]: Spot checks on U‑NEXT's own pages found films it includes that the data misses, so 1,863 is a floor.

[^paytv]: Pay-TV apps count as subscriptions, add-on listings included. Most of Movistar TV's 1,924 need a Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max or Paramount+ add-on or bundle.

[^seasons]: A show counts if any part of it is on a subscription. That doesn't guarantee every season.
