YepWatch support

Updated 11 November 2026

Get help

Email hello@goodwatch.app, or use Send feedback in the app. Include your device, operating system, YepWatch version and what happened. Please avoid sending passwords, invitation links or room credentials.

Using a room

Create a room, choose your country and services, and share the invitation with your friends. Each person chooses Yep or Nope privately. Open Matches to propose something and confirm it together. Watching takes place on the streaming service and may require its own subscription.

Connections and availability

If a room cannot reconnect, check your connection and reopen the app. Keep the original room rather than submitting a new one repeatedly. Availability can change by country or service. If a room has expired, start a new room.

Your data

Delete your YepWatch account and associated data at https://yepwatch.com/delete-account or from Profile. There is a 30-day recovery window before permanent removal. GoodWatch is separate and remains intact. Read https://yepwatch.com/privacy for details.
