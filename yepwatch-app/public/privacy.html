YepWatch privacy policy

Updated 11 November 2026

Who operates YepWatch

YepWatch is operated by Alper Ortac, Germany, the operator of GoodWatch. Contact hello@goodwatch.app for privacy questions or requests. Operator contact details are at https://goodwatch.app/disclaimer. This policy covers the YepWatch website and its Android and iPhone/iPad apps.

What YepWatch keeps

We keep the name you choose, your room memberships, viewing ideas, selected country and streaming services, private Yep/Nope choices, proposals, agreements and confirmed decisions. Room invitations and device credentials let you return to your place in a room. To enforce the allowance of 10 rooms created at a time, we also keep a random app-installation or browser identifier, or a pseudonymous account identifier when signed in. Server allowance records expire with the rooms after six hours and are then removed by routine cleanup; they do not contain your viewing choices. The random identifier stays in protected app storage, or in a browser cookie for 30 days. Your device also remembers rooms, your name and recent decisions.

If you connect GoodWatch, YepWatch receives an account identifier, your display name, saved country and streaming services, and the viewing preferences/progress needed for the features you use. Your GoodWatch password and email address are not passed to YepWatch. Deleting YepWatch data does not delete or modify your GoodWatch account or viewing records.

How the information is used

We use this information to run your rooms, find suggestions, identify shared matches, confirm agreement, recover your place and show decision history. Individual Yep/Nope choices are private from other people in the room. Your chosen name, room ideas, services and participation, shared matches and confirmed choices can be seen by people with access to the room.

Viewing ideas are sent to the GoodWatch search service for interpretation, which may use AI processing. Do not put sensitive personal information in viewing ideas. We process data needed to provide the service you request and use necessary security information to keep it reliable and prevent abuse.

Other services and network requests

Catalog artwork is loaded from image providers, including TMDB. Opening a YouTube trailer loads a third-party player. Google can process your IP address, device/browser identifiers and playback activity, use cookies for measurement and advertising, and infer a general location from your IP address according to YouTube and Google policies. The country used for YepWatch suggestions is the streaming region you choose; YepWatch does not request device location permission. Opening a streaming link takes you to that service and its privacy policy.

The installed apps contact Expo for compatible app updates. These requests include a random installation identifier, IP address, platform/runtime details and update identifiers. Failed launches can also send failed-update identifiers and a short launch-error message. Expo uses these records to deliver updates, count installations and diagnose failed updates on our behalf, without advertising tracking. Expo deletes raw launch-error logs after three days. Hosting and network providers process connection information such as IP addresses to serve requests. Support emails contain what you choose to send, plus app/version details when you use Send feedback.

Storage and security

Native app credentials are stored using the operating system’s protected storage. Browser sign-in credentials and the random room-allowance identifier use HttpOnly cookies; room reminders and recent decisions use browser session storage. These stores support the features you request. Access to server data and backups is restricted to operators who need it.

Room access expires with the room. Account decision history has a separate lifetime and remains until you request deletion. Network/security logs and support correspondence are retained only as needed for reliability, security and responding to your request. Service providers may process data outside your country under their applicable transfer safeguards.

Delete and recover your YepWatch data

Use Profile → Delete YepWatch account, or visit https://yepwatch.com/delete-account. Sign in to identify your YepWatch account. Guest data can be deleted from the device/browser where it was created. You do not need to reinstall the app to use the web deletion page.

Deletion immediately removes your access to your YepWatch rooms and hides local profile/history. For 30 days, retained data can be recovered using the explicit Restore action. Signing in alone does not cancel deletion. After that recovery deadline, an automated cleanup permanently removes your YepWatch account links, private room records and identity from shared decision history. Other participants keep their own decisions. Expired rooms cannot be reopened by restoration.

Recovery data on a device is removed when that device next runs the app after the deadline. Restricted backups may retain copies until their normal rotation; they are not used to restore a deleted account. Your GoodWatch account is separate and remains intact.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your personal data, or object to eligible processing, by contacting hello@goodwatch.app. Where processing relies on consent you may withdraw it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Uninstalling the app does not itself request server-side deletion.

YepWatch is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us. We update this page when our practices change and show the revision date above.
