Using YepWatch

YepWatch helps people decide which movie or show to watch together. It does not supply the movies or shows. The service is currently free; streaming providers may require a separate subscription. You are responsible for using those services according to their own terms.

Rooms and content

Share invitations only with people you want in the room. Use names and viewing ideas responsibly, respect others’ rights, and do not include unlawful or sensitive personal content. You can leave a room at any time. Availability and catalog information may be incomplete or change.

Accounts and deletion

GoodWatch sign-in is optional. Your YepWatch data and the GoodWatch account used for sign-in are separate. You can request deletion of YepWatch data from Profile or https://yepwatch.com/delete-account, with a 30-day recovery window followed by permanent removal.
