Everyone’s Yep.

Help test the free YepWatch Android app. Create a room, invite your friends and swipe Yep or Nope until you find something you all want to watch.

Use the same Google account for all three steps and in the Play Store on your Android phone.

  1. Join the tester group

    Open YepWatch Testers and choose Join group. This gives your Google account access to the closed test.

    Join the group ↗

  2. Become a tester

    Open the Google Play testing page and choose Become a tester. Joining the group alone does not enroll you.

    Become a tester ↗

  3. Install from Google Play

    Once the testing page says You are a tester, install YepWatch on your Android phone.

    Get YepWatch ↗

App not available? Check that all three pages and your Play Store use the same Google account. Allow a little time after joining, then reopen the testing link.

Try a room with a friend, vote Yep/Nope and agree on a movie or show. Send feedback in the app or email hello@goodwatch.app.
