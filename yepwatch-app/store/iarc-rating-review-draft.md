# USK / IARC rating review draft

Prepared for the owner to review. This message has not been sent.

To: iarc@usk.de
Subject: Clarification / rating review for YepWatch (com.yepwatch.app)

Hello,

Please review the questionnaire interpretation and USK rating for YepWatch:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yepwatch.app
Developer: GoodWatch; contact: hello@goodwatch.app.

YepWatch helps an invited private group choose a movie or TV show using Yep/Nope votes. It displays catalog titles, summaries, posters and age certifications, and links to the streaming providers. It does not sell or play the full films/shows. User-authored content is limited to names and viewing ideas in invited rooms; there is no public feed.

The replacement build 19 opens trailers in the external YouTube app/browser and has no embedded playback or advertising. Older builds included YouTube trailer embeds. Adult-flagged catalog titles are excluded, but the catalog is not restricted to child-rated films.

The current rating is USK 16, with “Content for different age groups” and “Users interact.” The questionnaire includes promoted online content, which we have not omitted. We would like clarification on whether its violence, nudity and language questions refer to the catalog artwork/text actually displayed by this discovery tool, the external films it links to, or both. In particular, how should the distinction between visual depictions and references to products be answered when the full works are never played in YepWatch?

Please advise the correct questionnaire treatment and review whether the calculated rating accurately describes this functionality. We are seeking an accurate classification, not an override of content that must be declared.

Thank you,
Alper Ortac / GoodWatch
