# Episode presentation preserves watch identity

MF GHOST has one regular season in TMDB and three in IMDb, although both catalogs refer to the same 37 episodes. Selected shows use a reviewed, versioned episode presentation shared by ratings and tracking; watched history and imports keep canonical TMDB identities and positions. Changing stored numbering would reinterpret old watches and bulk actions, so displayed-season Mark and Unmark instead validate explicit episode IDs against that presentation. Mapping approval requires one-to-one identities under the same IMDb parent, agreement on episode granularity and Specials, and preserved canonical order; trailing unreviewed episodes have an Additional episodes row. General provider regrouping remains opt-in because counts and group completeness cannot prove those conditions.
