# YepWatch room ideas and streaming union

Status: owner-approved 2026-11-01; implemented on `feat/yepwatch-room-redesign`, not deployed. Publish-first ideas: owner decision Q32, 2026-11-12.

This supersedes the description, service selection and deck exhaustion rules in [the earlier room-description decision](yepwatch-shared-room-description.md). Leave, participant identity, account grants and proposal anonymity retain their established meanings. The implementation follows [the room redesign specification](../../yepwatch-app/docs/room-redesign.md).

A room shares up to eight ideas, each with its author, interpretation and host-controlled on/off switch. Anyone may suggest; host suggestions start on, others start off. Authors edit their own ideas; the host may remove any idea. Ideas publish first (owner decision Q32, 2026-11-12): create with a first idea, add and edit store the idea pending and answer at once; its search runs afterwards and lands as a later committed revision. The command that stores the idea also reserves one of the room's 24 search slots in the same commit, and that slot is spent whatever happens next (owner decision 2026-11-12): an answer, a failure, a refusal for GoodWatch's spend limit, or an edit or removal while pending. Nothing is ever given back; 24 per room is plenty, and refund accounting across takeovers was where the bugs were. The cap is on reservations, not on search attempts: one reservation may take several attempts. Each attempt holds the idea's lease under a random job id stored on the idea, and only the job holding it lands a result. A pending idea whose 15 s lease ran out (crashed or restarted instance, found by a read or the API's once-a-minute sweep) is taken over under a new job and searched again on the same reservation; an edit stores a new job, so the replaced search's answer never lands. Each API process runs at most 3 background searches at once (GoodWatch search admits about 4 in flight) and queues the rest, claiming the lease only when an attempt starts. GoodWatch busy or rate limited keeps the idea pending and is retried later with a per-room backoff; other failures end it uninterpreted. The sweep pages through rooms with a wrapping cursor, so rooms that keep failing never hold the others back. The retained draft-preview endpoint spends from the same 24. Restart does not renew that budget. Search results are immutable idea records referenced by the room head, rather than ranked lists embedded in that head.

Each present participant contributes their selected services. A null pick contributes no services and still counts fully for voting and agreement. The room's enabled selection is the union minus the host's disabled services. While choosing, a settings command cannot remove the last enabled service. Country remains shared. A guest host may initially have no nickname; Start requires a nickname and an enabled service.

Ideas, services and kinds change without restarting a round. Unanswered cards that no longer qualify are removed and their canonical keys become eligible for delivery again. Any answer record, including an undo, retains the card and its votes. Candidate idea attribution is a set: removing one idea does not remove a title still wanted by another enabled idea. Matches and proposals become unavailable when their title no longer meets the active idea/kind/service selection; retained historical answers remain private.

The deck interleaves enabled ideas and falls back to popularity when a lane runs out. With no enabled ideas it uses popularity directly. The 120 active-card and 2000 seen-key limits remain server resource bounds. They are quiet stops for the client, not completion screens or promises to enumerate an unlimited catalog.

Linked members default to excluding their GoodWatch seen titles. Only their own snapshot exposes their show-seen setting. The server reads seen titles through the existing account resource and read grant, caches by room/member/roster version, and refreshes when someone joins. Member-disabled releases have no remote member reader and perform no account reads.
