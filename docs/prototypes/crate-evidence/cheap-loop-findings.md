# Cheap search loop — current practical findings

**No overall winner is accepted.** Conservative catalog-identity cleanup is a narrow retained improvement. Pre-cap conjunction is promising on two original requests but fails supplemental generalization. The final normalization-only comparison is complete. The evidence supports stopping this bounded prototype loop with known tradeoffs, not claiming that every possible cheap algorithm is exhausted.

The same 13 original requests and all six original ranking lists remain the primary comparison. The fixed additional 28 requests are a separate, already-seen regression cohort. Neither cohort is silently replaced with easier questions.

## What the measured alternatives establish

Concrete prose centrality improves sunglasses and some driving requests. Clause boundaries repair a negation implementation error. Bounded shortlists can reduce preparation costs, but smaller pools sometimes lose useful candidates. Stock English phrase retrieval plus anti-compensation gives strong wealthy-subject comedy candidates; universal centrality/routing still regresses relations and mood requests. Familiar-title retention is not a quality score: unfamiliar titles with direct source evidence are accepted.

Native SQL field boosts, best-fields and phrase shapes were measured against fresh controls. They yield some credible car/getaway lists but do not universally fix wealthy-subject conjunction or indirect narrator sense. Broad dictionary expansion leaves 12/13 top-five lists unchanged and adds a wrong-sense eating-monster result on the only changed case. It also adds substantial local work.

Pre-cap SQL conjunction addresses a real truncation problem. On dark comedy about rich people, its anti64 top five contains Saltburn, The Righteous Gemstones, The Raspberry Reich, The White Lotus and The Favourite. Four are strong wealth/class matches; the revolutionary-gang focus of The Raspberry Reich remains a weakness. On getaway driving, Drive, Baby Driver and Motorway fit directly, although the original alternatives were already strong. Matched five-repeat retrieval medians improve from about 825 to 616 ms for wealthy comedy and 866 to 533 ms for getaway; separately measured local anti work is about 30 and 3 ms. These sums are reconstructions, not measured full-search latency.

The supplemental cohort prevents accepting that route as a general default. Older-robbery fallback prioritizes The Pink Panther, Snatch and Shazam! without the required older-robber subject. Peaceful-alien communication admits Mister Rogers and PAW Patrol; anti reranking also exceeds 50 ms locally there. Nature-documentary output is credible but the baseline was already good. A legal fallback adds work without a quality gain. Most other requests stay unchanged, which is not proof their baseline results are good.

Same-budget interpretation trials likewise do not earn adoption. Revised semantic wording gates away the long-tense lexical path and shifts indirect-narrator results toward time-travel/investigation without direct narrator evidence. Candidate-generation-only wording mostly preserves output; its getaway reorder is a credible tradeoff rather than a clear quality win. Budget compliance and better-looking extracted phrases do not establish better recommendations.

Conservative source-identity cleanup succeeds within ordinary film/series scope. The whole 50,000-row audit identifies three explicit attraction experiences and one standalone nonreleased pilot, while retaining ride adaptations, location mentions, released-series pilot history and ambiguous shorts. Applied separately, it changes only car/narrator lists and supplies relevant replacements. It does not solve the broader ranking problem or imply attraction works lack value.

Normalization-only review froze original phrase polarity and selected identities in both arms. Snowball stemming repairs real equivalences: Arrival rises to the top of the general peaceful-contact list; restaurant-workplace and medical-comedy leaders also improve. But a car-pursuit request now leads with Heartstopper (`movie:10124`, the institutional slasher), The Bayou and Fear PHarm, whose sources describe hospital, plane-crash and corn-maze horror rather than drivers escaping in cars. In the conjunction pool, Arrival rises to second but Buzz Lightyear and PAW Patrol still occupy first/third; local work is about 86 ms. These mixed results reject the current universal stemmed ranker. This is not a claim of exact Lucene analyzer parity.

The loop has tested distinct retrieval, lexical, centrality, weighting, shortlist, dictionary, prompt, catalog-hygiene and structural-conjunction mechanisms. These findings justify a practical stop for this measured prototype: useful mechanisms have been isolated, and tested refinements still introduce material regressions or violate the overhead target. More arbitrary combinations are not supported by a new general improvement hypothesis. They cannot prove that every possible cheap search algorithm has been exhausted. Any retained change must still pass the original-request regression and cost gates; no title-specific or request-specific exceptions are accepted as a general solution.
