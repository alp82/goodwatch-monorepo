Does the buzz score find what was actually talked about?

Throwaway logic/data spike for GitHub #543. Compare six UTC days against dated releases, trailers and news. This measures article attention, not social mentions, sentiment or viewing recommendations.

500 catalog titles (67 movies, 433 shows); 431 unambiguous English Wikipedia sitelinks; 425 complete 60-day histories. Scores cover September 23–October 13, 2026. Tables below cover October 8–13.

Recommended starting rule for sustained current attention: rank by views / max(μ28, 1), with views ≥ 1,000 and views − μ28 ≥ 500. For a “hot” badge also require lift ≥ 2; do not fill 20 slots with weak signals. Retain z-score as a burst diagnostic. Keep TMDB and Netflix as dated supporting reasons until prospective trending snapshots can validate a blend. Thresholds are provisional.

Clock limits: TMDB daily export popularity is a historical proxy, not trending. The trending table deliberately reuses the October 14 snapshot on earlier days: HINDSIGHT ONLY, excluded from the recommendation. Its 100 raw rows per type contain 98 movie / 91 show unique IDs; the first observed position wins. Netflix uses the latest available TSV week with assumed Tuesday publication (week-ending Sunday + 2 days), never the scored week before publication; the downloaded file ends October 4. Wikipedia is last-complete-day data. No current partial UTC day is scored.

Reading the evidence: “direct” means a dated event for that work, not proven pageview causation. Deaths, anniversaries, franchise context, anticipation and inferences are labeled. “No dated event verified” is an explicit research gap, not evidence nothing happened. Evidence was checked October 14; later reporting can explain an earlier event and never enters the numeric score.

Coverage & limits

Fixed cohort selected using current catalog TMDB popularity, with no presentability/adult filter; survivorship and show-heavy selection bias remain. Missing dates are never filled with zero. Ambiguous Wikidata article matches are excluded. English reading traffic undersamples non-English audiences. Redirect traffic is not consolidated. Netflix matching is exact normalized title plus movie/show category and unique only within this cohort; it is provisional. Only 5 titles matched recent Netflix TSV history, and only The Gentlemen and Raw matched its latest week.

TMDB export dates are morning snapshots, not full UTC-day event counts. Wikipedia and TMDB were collected later; historical values can be revised. Netflix replay assumes Tuesday availability and has no archived publication vintages. The formula weights and floors are hypotheses, not learned parameters. No production write, cache access, app server, deployment or publication was performed.

* TMDB z uses denominator max(SD28, 0.05 × mean28, 0.1). Pageviews+TMDB uses 75% capped positive article z + 25% capped positive TMDB z. Adding Netflix holds the 75% / 25% base fixed and adds a bonus of 0.1 × (11 − best category/season rank), so any rank change is attributable to Netflix. Capping is 0–10. Lift is views / max(mean28, 1). Trending hindsight uses 80% capped positive article z + 20% of 10 × (101 − rank)/100, ranked separately for movies/shows. These blends are deliberately simple sensitivity comparisons.
