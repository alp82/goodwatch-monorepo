How to capture

  1. Open your Watchlist on Prime Video and refresh the tab. Don't scroll yet. Leave the filter on All.
  2. Choose Start capture. It keeps running after this popup closes.
  3. Scroll slowly to the very end of the Watchlist, until no more titles load.
  4. Open this popup again and choose Stop and save files.
What this does and doesn't do

It reads the Watchlist only in this tab, only after you choose Start. It reads the page's own data and the title cards you scroll past, and copies of the list data the page loads while you scroll.

It doesn't scroll, click, open pages, send anything anywhere, read cookies or change your Watchlist.

It saves two files: the wishlist file for GoodWatch, and a report with counts and field names only, without titles or IDs.
