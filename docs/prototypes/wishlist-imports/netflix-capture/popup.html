How to capture

  1. In this tab, open My List on netflix.com with your profile. If you updated this extension, refresh the tab first.
  2. Choose Start capture. It keeps running after this popup closes.
  3. Grid: scroll slowly to the end. Row named My List or Meine Liste: click that row's next arrow (point at the row to show it) until titles repeat. Don't use other rows' arrows.
  4. Open this popup again and choose Stop and save file.

GoodWatch doesn't match these titles on its own. On the Imports page, choose Match titles and pick each one. Unmatched titles aren't added.

More about this capture

This version reads the current Netflix My List layout. Live capture on Netflix is still unverified.

The file doesn't say whether a title is a movie or a show, so GoodWatch doesn't match its titles on its own. When you upload it on GoodWatch's Imports page, choose Match titles and pick the right GoodWatch title for each one you want. Titles you don't match aren't added.

This popup closes when you click the page, but the capture keeps running. The My List row can wrap around to its first titles, so stop when titles start repeating.

The count includes titles that Netflix loaded on the page while capturing, including some that may be off screen. Netflix may hide titles that aren't available in your country.

This works only in this tab. Opening this popup checks the tab for a running capture. Titles are read only after you choose Start capture. It doesn't scroll, click, open pages, or send anything anywhere. The file holds titles and Netflix links only, with no cookies or account details. It doesn't change your Netflix list.
