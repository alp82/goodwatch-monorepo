## Fix in a later respin (none blocking; all fail closed)

1. **A committed preflight handoff can go unreported.** If `consumerBirth`, `validateAdmissionFrame` or `preflightEvidence` throws after preflight exits 0, the on-disk handoff has advanced. The `failed` row carries neither the new SHA nor a written flag. The `cpu0_control_cleanup_timeout` path is similar: it reports `handoffAccepted:false` even if a `handoff-complete` frame was already read. This is not false success, but the uncertainty is implicit. Project something like `handoffWriteObserved` and the SHA.

2. **Some cleanup-uncertainty facts are still dropped.** `failureEvidence` does not project these, so `cleanupObserved:false` is the only signal:
   - `cleanupReason` and `pinChildExitCode` from the `released` frame;
   - `childFailureDetails` (`releaseState.heldMappingCount`);
   - `cachePreparationFailure.writerFailure` (`cleanupUnknown`, `pidfdRetained`);
   - `cpuHierarchySnapshotFailure`.
   
   After a readiness `ContractFailure` the reader loop has ended, so a later `released` frame is never collected and `cleanupObserved` is always false there. The CPU0-CONSUMER.md phrase "cleanup/finalization evidence" overstates this slightly.

3. **Several refusal reasons collapse to `cpu0_comparison_refused`.** The `publicFailure` allowlist lacks `cpu0_preflight_refused`, `cpu0_preflight_invalid` and `cpu0_consumer_birth_invalid`. Preflight cases are distinguishable only by `failureEvidence` versus `evidenceUnavailable`. Raw `/proc` errors from `consumerBirth` are also generic.

4. **`exeHash` uses double-precision stat fields.** An inode above 2^53 would break equality with Python's exact integers and refuse every run, after the handoff write. Same-filesystem overlay2 on ext4 or xfs is unlikely to hit this; `statSync(path,{bigint:true})` removes the risk.

5. **Minor:**
   - `audit_cpu0.mjs` still compares `process.argv[1]` without `realpathSync`, so a symlinked invocation exits 0 with no output.
   - A non-object controller frame (for example `null`) makes `failureEvidence` throw inside the catch in `controlledBatch`, losing the exit facts.
   - `evidenceRejected` from a nested `pressureFinalization` is not raised to the top level.
   - The oversize-response path in the transport has no fixture.
   - `socketClosed` is a literal after the await rather than derived from `socket.destroyed`.

## External integration prerequisites

- **Birth registration:** the outer pipe must run `validateAdmissionFrame` and independently confirm the birth. `outsideSourceCgroup` and the group-hash inequality are self-reported and do not prove non-descendance.
- **After any refusal or timeout:** the outer must re-read the on-disk handoff rather than trust its last known SHA (see item 1).
- **Cleanup audit:** writer and holder disappearance stays with the external audit. The auditor's `consumerPass` only, `experimentPass:false` and `externalCleanupRequired:true` are intact.
- **Audit context:** `initialHandoff` must arrive with `sha256` attached. The on-disk handoff must be exactly `canonical(record)+"\n"`.
- **Stage:** it must be fresh for each run. The controller creates `pin-instance.lock` and writer receipts there, and the inventory is checked once at entry.
- **Budgets and frame bound:** the 15 s and 5 s post-ack budgets and the 131072-byte frame bound are unchanged and unproven for CPU0, as you stated. A timeout sends SIGTERM that can land during `commit_handoff`; the commit is atomic, but its outcome is then unknown to the consumer.
- **Outer pins:** Node binary and provenance, the real producer exit and stage permissions remain outer responsibilities.
