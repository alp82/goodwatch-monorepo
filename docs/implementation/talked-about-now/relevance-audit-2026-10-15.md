## Editorial rubric and limits

Relevant means a substantive official first look/clip/creative asset; title-focused original review, analysis, interview or concrete news; or a worthwhile social contribution backed by actual engagement or an established critic/journalist/outlet/participant. Fresh criticism of an old movie can qualify. A dated restoration, new production disclosure, creator memoir revelation, or current cast tribute can qualify; film release age alone is not the rule.

Not relevant means routine sales copy, an unsupported one-line reaction, a viewing log, résumé/comparator mention, archive scene without a new hook, recycled availability content, generic engagement prompt, or a redundant representative of already covered material. Set-level duplicates are counted as not relevant even when the underlying article is respectable. Two journalist shares qualify intrinsically but duplicate original RSS articles; P243/P244 therefore count as duplicate rejections, leaving six useful Bluesky representatives rather than eight.

Every piece was reviewed from stored text, excerpt/context, title association and provenance. All Bluesky pieces additionally have public profile/post evidence when available. Selected suspicious editorial pages were opened; **not every full article was read, and no video was watched**. YouTube verdicts assess the evidenced caption/description/format; “thin promo” means the available evidence does not establish a worthwhile asset, not a claim about unseen frames. Borderline format decisions require a later human sample review.

PR #596 makes new matching deterministic but deliberately preserves old reviewed links and does not bump the shared matcher version. The corpus therefore still includes legacy peripheral associations. These are judged for current usefulness, **not presented as evidence that the new deterministic matcher produced them**, and no matching changes are proposed here. The same post can be relevant for one associated title and irrelevant for another.

## Signals available without added cost

Existing [social schema](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/d3867c107388e7370be9e25fcbf42203e4400b0b/goodwatch-flows/windmill/f/social/schema.py) stores post/source/author IDs, URL, publication/discovery/check times, text/context/provenance, hashes, link lane and duplicate group. Quality evidence has URLs/card text plus minimal profile age/post-count/bot evidence. **It stores no likes, reposts, quotes, replies, follower counts or video views. No outlet tiers or known-person registry exist today.**

- **Available now:** source publication age, editorial hostname, ten verified YouTube channel IDs, current content-quality decision, title metadata, linked article URLs, textual fingerprints and current duplicate groups. Existing duplicates primarily group YouTube trailers; every non-YouTube post normally gets its own group, so RSS/social copies are not collapsed.
- **Free, already returned by existing APIs:** Bluesky `getPosts` has like/repost/quote/reply counts; `getProfiles` has followers. The current adapter discards them. [First-party post schema](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/packages/api/lexicons/app/bsky/feed/defs.json), [profile schema](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/packages/api/lexicons/app/bsky/actor/defs.json). Mastodon similarly exposes [status counts](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/entities/Status/) and [account followers](https://docs.joinmastodon.org/entities/Account/); zero Mastodon links in this audit means its thresholds have no measured local validation.
- **Free with a configured key:** YouTube [videos.list](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos/list) exposes [statistics](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/videos#statistics) at one quota unit per call. Production uses keyless official feeds, so the proposal does not require video counts or invent a current threshold measurement. Official asset provenance plus substance can qualify without likes.
- **Free to derive/fetch, not currently complete:** publisher `datePublished`/`dateModified`, explicit republication notices, canonical URLs, low-substance content class, and stable account-ID role registration. Do not infer that a modified date is a new publication or that a bio/follower count proves someone is a critic. The selected four republication checks are not an exhaustive crawl.

## Concrete deterministic candidate and measured effect

**These are proposals, not production changes.** Preserve all current identity, hash, content-quality, report and deletion safeguards. Add a separate relevance decision. The following conservative replay uses stored evidence plus the free Bluesky hydration. No inference, paid service, webapp writes or job reruns are needed for the replay.

The measured candidate is deliberately explicit and imperfect. Standalone counts apply each rule to all 632 original associations. Cumulative counts apply the rules in the listed order. Source-specific rules leave other sources untouched. Zero coverage always uses all 20 ranked titles, including the three empty before filtering.

| Step | Rule | Alone keep / drop | Alone zero /20 | Additional dropped in sequence | Cumulative keep | Cumulative zero /20 | Human-relevant retained |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | Freshness: news/social 7 days; video 14 days | 623 / 9 | 3 (15%) | 9 | 623 | 3 (15%) | 320 |
| 2 | YouTube: substantive official formats | 586 / 46 | 3 (15%) | 46 | 577 | 3 (15%) | 315 |
| 3 | RSS: outlet tier + editorial type | 564 / 68 | 3 (15%) | 66 | 511 | 3 (15%) | 292 |
| 4 | RSS: exact foreground name + personal-news exclusions | 532 / 100 | 3 (15%) | 67 | 444 | 3 (15%) | 260 |
| 5 | Social: known author or measured reach | 505 / 127 | 5 (25%) | 127 | 317 | 5 (25%) | 260 |
| 6 | Duplicate collapse | 592 / 40 | 3 (15%) | 19 | 298 | 5 (25%) | 257 |

1. **Publication recency:** require 0–7 days for social and editorial items; 0–14 days for verified official video assets. Use `published_at`, never discovery or last-seen time. This rejects nine RSS associations here and no social/video associations. A flat seven-day window would reject 18 associations, including nine videos, unnecessarily emptying the Dragon Ball title. This window does not solve republished articles or freshly uploaded archive scenes; separate those below.
2. **Official video ≠ any official upload:** require one of the ten existing stable channel IDs and a substantive format in the headline: trailer, teaser, sneak peek, first look, featurette, interview, behind-the-scenes, camera test, music/lyric video, filmed performance, or explicitly described scene/sketch/stand-up material. Short leading descriptions may establish an actual scene or craft feature. Reject trailer countdowns and game activations. For an older catalog title require a dated current season/release/restoration/anniversary hook; mere upload recency is insufficient. Do not let boilerplate cast/synopsis/subscribe text supply substance. Exact replay predicates appear in the appendix. The format rule alone keeps 33/79 video links; later duplicate collapse keeps 30/79.
3. **Editorial source and type:** define explicit tiers. Tier A: Variety, Deadline, THR, IndieWire, Guardian, BBC, RogerEbert, epd Film, film-rezensionen. Tier B: Collider, Screen Rant, /Film, Den of Geek, Filmstarts, Moviepilot. These are proposed operational tiers, not an objective quality ranking. Reject headline patterns for routine availability/TV schedules, giveaways, merchandise, red-carpet galleries, generic lists and replacement bait. Tier A then qualifies provisionally; Tier B additionally requires an explicit review/interview/reported event/production/launch indicator. Official Netflix press must announce a concrete release/asset/event. Trust is a provenance input; it cannot excuse peripheral celebrity stories or all recycled news.
4. **Foreground relationship, not merely correct identity:** the replay uses a deliberately strict fallback: normalized catalog title or original title must occur as a whole phrase in the headline, and routine management signings, jury appointments, celebrity trials, merchandise/gala/insurance stories are excluded. **Do not ship raw headline equality as the final relevance rule.** It loses translated/shorthand titles and genuinely substantive body-focused coverage. Use the existing deterministic mention/alias evidence to identify the actual review/news subject, and require a specific current event or original analysis when a title is only in the excerpt. That richer variant has not been replayed here; do not quote the table’s counts as its predicted output.
5. **Social reach or reviewed authority, plus substance:** `E = likes + reposts + quotes`, excluding replies. Unknown accounts need **(E ≥ 20 and followers ≥ 500) or E ≥ 100**. Allow reviewed stable IDs for recognized critics/journalists/outlets/cast/crew to qualify without a reach floor, but apply content and foreground checks to everyone. Reject generic watch-location/recycled-scene reply prompts even when they pass reach. Missing metrics fail closed for the unknown-author lane while enrichment is pending; missing is never recorded as zero. For article shares by unknown accounts, require at least 12 own words after removing URL/hashtags, copied card headline/description and title tokens; this proposed extra predicate was not included in the measured replay and needs a separately reported count. Trusted authors can share their own reporting with fewer words.
6. **Duplicate groups before selection:** canonicalize article host/path, strip only known tracking keys/fragments, collapse same-title cross-source article copies, and prefer original RSS unless social text adds a substantive independent contribution. For trailers, group by title + trailer/teaser + cut within 72 hours, preferring English for the English surface and retaining the only localized version when no English version exists. For editorial headlines use same-title normalized token Jaccard ≥0.8 within 72 hours; also collapse explicitly recognized repeated trailer/first-look/first-reaction, Critics Choice nominations and IDFA-lineup event classes, protecting original reviews/interviews/analytical angles. Never use a generic one-story-per-title limit. The sample has seven exact canonical-article groups: 18 associations → seven representatives (11 drops). Including trailer/headline/event rules gives 40 standalone drops, 19 after the other gates. Different release/cut/event dates must remain distinct; broad event heuristics need fixtures for exceptions.

After the full conservative sequence: **298 retained / 334 dropped**. Of the retained associations, **257 match the chosen relevant representatives, 41 fail those labels**; **70 of 327 worthwhile associations are lost**. This is **86.2% relevance agreement and 78.6% retention of worthwhile associations on this same audited dataset**, not independent validation. Some apparent duplicate “errors” are alternate respectable representatives where the replay chose a different outlet than the manual review. Other errors are actual peripheral material or uncaught repeat coverage. Do not report all retained articles as proven good.

For the **top 20** specifically, the replay retains **38 links: 37 matching the chosen relevant set, one alternate repeated-event representative**, leaving five titles empty. That is 97.4% agreement for the top-20 retained set, but it misses 16 of the 53 useful top-20 associations. For all titles, retained-source counts are **30 YouTube (30 relevant), 261 RSS (221 relevant, 40 not), seven Bluesky associations (six relevant, one incidental comparator)**. The remaining social comparator is Bouie/Jurassic World; authority is not enough.

Additional rules with bounded evidence:

- **Republication freshness:** if publisher HTML explicitly discloses a republication and there is no separately dated substantive update, reject it as “now” content. Four verified examples would be rejected standalone; all four already fail other candidate gates, so zero additional drops in the measured sequence. Other pages were not exhaustively checked, so a corpus-wide keep count is unknown.
- **Linked-article freshness for social cards:** use cached original RSS/public `datePublished`, not the social post date; apply the editorial window. No old linked-article timestamp was verified in the Bluesky corpus, so there is no honest extra-drop estimate beyond zero verified cases. Publication metadata is required before claiming an item is old.
- **Quality-ranked selection, no source quota:** after eligibility/dedupe, show up to three pieces, with at most one trailer; rank substantive official assets, original reviews/reporting, then qualified independent commentary by authority/reach and recency. Do not reserve a social slot or fill a rejected slot just to achieve three items. Eligibility counts remain unchanged; current picker replay on the strict candidate selects 11 instead of 17 pieces. A new priority-ranking implementation was not built or tested.
