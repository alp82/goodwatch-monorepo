# GoodWatch matcher naming: SwipeWatch and alternatives

Checked **2026-10-20, 10:28–10:40 UTC**; individual volatile checks are timed below. Status: **preliminary naming research and recommendations, not a naming decision, reservation or legal clearance**.

## Verdict and ranked shortlist

**Recommend moving away from SwipeWatch, including the spaced “Swipe Watch” variant.** Google Play already lists **[SwipeWatch Couples Movie Night](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kamilnowak.swipewatch)** by **Kamil Nowak**, describing movie matching for couples/friends on the couch or in different cities. A second app, **[SwipeWatch — Movie Discovery](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.swipewatch.PhantomWorks)** by **PhantomWorks**, uses the same core name in Entertainment. These are practical discovery/confusion problems before any trademark conclusion. Capitalization or a space would not meaningfully distinguish the spoken name: that is a naming judgment, not a legal determination.

**Top picks to take into fuller screening: ReelNod, ScreenNod and ReelAccord.** All six finalists below had no exact product/app-title collision surface in the checks performed, both exact domains returned registry “not found” responses, and a public registrar displayed ordinary registration offers. Those three observations together make them better leads than an exact-string web search alone. **None is established as legally free or store-name-reservable.**

“Free alternatives” is interpreted as candidates without an observed exact competing product claim or premium acquisition requirement. **New domains still cost money.** At the quoted registrar, each finalist's `.com` was **US$11.08 for one year, renewing at US$11.08/year**; each `.app` was **US$8.75 for the introductory year, renewing at US$14.93/year**. These were name-specific public search offers, not purchases or guaranteed future prices. [Registrar's pricing terms](https://porkbun.com/products/domains) describe USD pricing for nonpremium domains; exact-name search links and times appear in the domain table.

## Product context and method

The intended app helps a partner/group choose something everyone wants to watch now, with couch, remote and mixed groups, optional initial GoodWatch login and a proposed browser guest path. Those settings/identity requirements are recorded in [the product research, lines 24–28](goodwatch-mobile-group-matcher.md#L24-L28). Movies-first remains a recommendation, so names should also work for shows. A name implying only a couch, exactly two people, swiping forever or synchronized video would unnecessarily narrow the confirmed product or promise an unapproved capability.

Read the repository's `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agents/{issue-tracker,triage-labels,domain}.md`, relevant `CONTEXT.md` vocabulary, the matcher note and [ADR 0010](../adr/0010-static-hostname-with-server-side-fallback.md). Existing naming notes and other research establish the standalone `docs/research/` convention. The checkout was `feat/shared-scoring`, HEAD `7444203f`, with extensive unrelated modifications already present. This research adds only this note; it does not update domain decisions or the product brief.

Methods: public official App Store/Google Play listings and searches, Apple's public software-search endpoint, first-party websites/source repositories, IANA-authorized registry RDAP endpoints, publicly rendered registrar search offers, USPTO's public search UI, EUIPO-coordinated TMview and an attempted UKIPO search. A normal headless Firefox browser was used when text-only requests returned JavaScript shells. No accounts, app records, carts, registrations, messages, purchases or reservations were created; no security challenge was solved or bypassed. No company was contacted. ## What the checks do and do not establish

The Apple results were often numerous and fuzzy, rather than literally empty; those returned titles were inspected for the candidate string. Play results ranged from other apps to empty result lists. Only SwipeWatch's Apple variants received the additional German/UK storefront queries. This is neither a worldwide store audit nor a name-reservation test, and delisted, unpublished, localized, unindexed and recently launched products may be missed.

Domain checks establish a point-in-time registry response and a public registrar offer, not ownership of the name, a successful checkout or a right to use it as a brand.
