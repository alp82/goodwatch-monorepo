# Movie coverage audit for the proposed fingerprint V2

## Scope and evidence

Audited the exact top **2,000 movie catalog rows**, ranked by GoodWatch overall vote count descending with stable TMDB-ID tie break. The root extraction manifest records SQL, timestamp and hash. All rows have a synopsis; 8 lack essence tags. Sources: [movie extract](top-movies.json), [extraction manifest](title-extraction-manifest.json), [compact registry](../../design/fingerprint-v2/llm-definition.json), [full registry](../../design/fingerprint-v2/definition.json).

Coverage consists of a six-field lexical scan of every row against 94 candidate families; manual screening of all 2,000 title names; reading the first four essence tags for all 798 rows outside the initial 63 candidate families; and substantive inspection of synopsis/tags/keywords for 77 selected titles. The initial residual pass introduced 31 further candidate families. This is a systematic catalog vocabulary audit with targeted editorial review, **not 2,000 independent deep movie reviews**. The [per-title ledger](movies-title-ledger.json) records the distinction, and [coverage JSON](movies-coverage.json) gives counts.

Existing essence tags and keywords are model/catalog evidence of represented concepts, not a verified reference answer about the actual work. No external factual verification or scene-level confirmation was performed. All changes below are proposals; definitions and production code remain untouched.

## Recommendation

Keep the stable scored profile comparatively small. Add an extensible, typed concept layer for precise species, sports, occupations, mechanisms, conditions and events. It must distinguish entity, activity, theme, genre, world mechanism and presentation roles, support canonical IDs and aliases, and record evidence/scope. This closes the long tail without a mandatory scalar for every animal, named religion or historical person.

The strongest omissions beyond a concept layer are explicit visual color mode; movie-level anthology and screenlife presentation; spatial restriction versus physical isolation; and descriptive dimensions for memory, ethics, confidence tricks and undercover operations. Corruption, body image and magical realism are additional candidates for fixed scored attributes, subject to annotation reliability and retrieval tests.

For a query such as “black and white car chase scenes,” explicit B&W and car-chase concepts are necessary, but independent title-level values do not establish that the chase itself is B&W. Store event relations/scopes where evidence exists, or return a co-occurrence uncertainty rather than claiming scene precision.

## False positives and existing coverage

- A “cult classic” is not evidence that a cult appears. The initial cult pattern matches 131 rows; this number must not become a factual cult-title count.
- The chess pattern finds seven rows, including figurative “chess match” language. The Seventh Seal synopsis gives a literal chess match; the other hits require independent checking.
- The bowling pattern finds The Big Lebowski, There Will Be Blood and Bowling for Columbine. A title-word match in the documentary does not establish a bowling-centered story.
- “Shrinking” and “climbing” can be metaphorical or psychological. “Shark” can mean a loan shark. “Wrestling” can mean emotional struggle. A word match only routes review.
- Satire, parody, noir, found family, revenge, grief, underdog arcs, school settings, jazz and many other frequent tags already have direct attributes. Do not add synonyms as extra dimensions.
- A named actor, director, adaptation, franchise, country, release decade or historical setting belongs in existing catalog/structured annotations. The fuller definition already handles holiday relations and animation technique; these are compact-contract integration needs, not missing scalar vocabulary.
- Freedom, mortality and purpose already appear explicitly in themes.existential_meaning. Their narrower child concepts help exact search without making a broad semantic-absence claim.
- Ballet is an activity, amateur/Olympic wrestling is a sport, professional wrestling combines performance and competition, and action martial arts is choreography. Cross-group leakage would reduce accuracy.

## Catalog and sampling limitations

- The exact ranked movie corpus contains movie rows titled Rick and Morty (rank 268, no essence tags) and Bojack Horseman (rank 1295, no essence tags), and duplicate-looking title entries. The audit preserves the requested ranking instead of silently changing its eligibility. The root source should be checked before production evaluation.
- Spider-Man (rank 83) contains a literal composer-name self-correction inside essence tags: “Daniel Lopatin-esque Score (Wait, no, Danny Elfman)”. This demonstrates why generated tags are evidence of vocabulary, not verified trait truth.
- Several upcoming/recent high-profile entries have rich editorial-looking metadata. This audit does not verify whether their popularity or descriptions represent actual viewer evidence.
- This high-vote sample favors widely seen movies; absence here does not prove a global-catalog concept is unimportant. Search phrase proposals are not measured demand or guarantees about scene facts.
- Narrative twists, transformations, cloning and death-game details can be spoilers. Use them for private retrieval without exposing explanatory spoilers by default.
