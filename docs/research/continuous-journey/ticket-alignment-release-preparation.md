## Recommendation

Keep the ticket and its existing split from [Deploy and verify the completed recommendation journey](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/issues/94). The preparation ticket correctly owns current operational facts, access, browser-tool readiness, and the actionable release/recovery runbook. The final release ticket correctly owns candidate-specific persisted-state compatibility, refreshed operational prerequisites, execution, and production acceptance. Both match the [confirmed acceptance resolution](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/issues/87#issuecomment-5721672847), which requires all agreed behavior to pass and forbids incidental stale account-data restoration during code rollback.

One small addition to the preparation ticket would make its completion evidence clearer:

> Record when and how each operational fact was verified, distinguishing current read-only observations from historical evidence and operator assertions. Identify recovery coverage and limitations separately for deployed code, configuration, browser-held progress, and server-held account/handoff state. Preparation identifies the recovery constraints; the final release task must prove compatibility against its actual candidate before deployment. If the candidate changes Windmill flows, include the main-branch flow deployment trigger and its recovery coordination in the runbook.

This is clarification, not a new dependency on implementing the final storage design. Do not move final compatibility proof into preparation or permit preparation alone to certify release readiness. Controlled account setup should remain coordinated with [Verify authenticated guest handoff and import recovery](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/issues/88), whose body already owns account-backed behavioral evidence and setup/cleanup; preparation establishes the access/tool prerequisites rather than duplicating that walkthrough.

## What remains unknown

Current Coolify app/environment and commit; automatic versus manual release trigger; deployment/configuration/recovery permissions; retained known-good artifact and configuration; exact restore/redeploy operation and prerequisites; whether staging exists; current browser/Lighthouse access; controlled account provisioning; and the eventual candidate's persisted-state changes all remain unverified by this bounded alignment review. These are already assigned to preparation or final release as appropriate. No historical credential result should be substituted for their verification.

Resolution of preparation should link an operational runbook with dated evidence and explicit limits. Resolution of final release must additionally demonstrate the actual candidate's local and production acceptance, compatible recovery preserving new activity, and fixture cleanup. An open access or QA prerequisite cannot be relabeled verified readiness, and a successfully recovered failed release cannot close the journey map under the confirmed acceptance resolution.
