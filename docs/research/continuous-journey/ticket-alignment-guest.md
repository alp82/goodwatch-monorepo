# Align shared guest progress and discovery continuity with the current implementation

Research date: 2026-09-17. Inspected working tree at `ef163b0ac13be83069fa042a192d274f35c85acf`, branch `prototype/connected-exploration`. This is source and decision research, not new browser verification. No tracker changes or implementation were made.

## Recommendation

Keep [Implement shared guest progress and discovery continuity](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/issues/89) open and retain its scope. Clarify its implementation starting point, acceptance cases, presentation boundary, and ownership of account handoff. The ticket is directionally correct; the accepted explore bar does not deliver shared guest persistence.

## Confirmed intent

[Decide how guest progress survives navigation and account creation](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/issues/84#issuecomment-5705214162) requires one replaceable guest interaction per title (rating, Want to See, or Skip), shared across Taste/details/Wishlist, persistence across visits in the same browser, and search/filter/sort/results/current-card continuity through navigation, reload and authentication. Twenty distinct currently rated titles is the cap; editing ratings, Want to See, Skip and browsing remain available. A dismissible reminder appears after ten ratings. Country/services survive, with account preference conflicts handled through explicit existing-account review.

[Choose the connected exploration and signup experience](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/issues/86#issuecomment-5721529691) accepted only incremental additions: real Taste-card links, the details explore bar, queue return, Previous/Next and refinement. Extra progress UI, prototype signup changes and separate preview action storage were rejected. The resolution explicitly leaves shared persistence and handoff open, so it does **not** repeal the reminder or persistence requirements; any additional presentation must respect the existing experience and receive live review where needed.

[Set production acceptance and release criteria for the journey](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/issues/87#issuecomment-5721672847) requires all agreed behavior on desktop/mobile, including member regressions; production build, no new TypeScript diagnostics and comparable Lighthouse evidence are release gates. [Webapp instructions](../../../goodwatch-webapp/AGENTS.md#L13) require browser QA and prohibit automated tests.

## Proposed ticket amendment

Append a compact “Starting point and boundaries” section:

> The accepted details explore bar and Taste-card links already exist at revision ef163b0, with loaded-pool refinement and tab-local return context. Extend the existing behavior; do not recreate rejected preview storage, progress panels, signup redesigns or mini detail dialogs. Implement the confirmed reminder incrementally; presentation requiring a user decision remains subject to live review.
>
> Reuse or safely migrate `onboarding_ratings`, preserving movie/show identity and one replaceable interaction per guest title. Connect guest-aware action reads/writes and hydrate Wishlist title data; verify the existing member Wishlist rendering as part of journey acceptance. Coordinate the storage, browser-preference, origin-context and successful-transfer cleanup contract with account-transfer delivery; review/import/recovery implementation stays there.
>
> Acceptance must include cross-page immediate visibility, same-browser subsequent visits, ordinary reload and back/return behavior beyond the existing Taste resume link, direct arrivals, and existing members. At twenty current ratings only a new rating prompts signup: editing a rated title, replacing its interaction, Want to See, Skip and browsing remain usable. Verify reminder dismissal, browser-storage failure/corruption without lost navigation, and preserved URL filters/sorting/results position for each relevant entry point. Do not mistake the existing sessionStorage queue for general journey continuity.

Storage-failure/corruption checks are a proposed robustness addition, not an already agreed separate product feature. A complete cross-tab synchronization promise is likewise not present in the decisions and should not be silently added.

## Confidence and limits

High confidence in the policy alignment and cited structural mismatches. Medium confidence about their runtime impact: this report did not use personal or controlled accounts, mutate production, run a browser walkthrough, run tests, or establish deployed revision. Existing prototype browser evidence applies only to the narrow accepted bar. The apparent member Wishlist defect warrants a focused runtime reproduction before choosing its exact fix.
