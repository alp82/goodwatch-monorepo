# GoodWatch paid-feature parity: comparison and build foundation

**Research snapshot: 2026-10-19. Status: research and proposals, not a release or approved campaign.**

GoodWatch can pursue a broad free alternative to useful paid movie/TV tools, but the strongest starting point is **portable history, flexible lists, and dependable return-use features**. Current main already contains substantial discovery, taste, tracking, import and MCP code. It does not yet establish complete export, general private lists, annual/all-time watch analytics, external calendars, outbound alerts, or continuous scrobbling/sync. No GoodWatch feature was independently verified live in this research. That blocks external parity claims, not completion of this comparison.

“Major” below is an explicit prioritization judgment: a capability is important when it preserves a user's accumulated data, solves a recurring movie/TV task, appears across several independently documented paid offers, or enables a credible switch from a particular product. Frequency in this inventory is evidence of recurring product positioning, **not proof of demand, popularity, market share, or willingness to switch**. Cosmetic/supporter rewards and professional/media-playback services remain visible without being confused with functional consumer gaps.

## Coverage, identity and confidence

The canonical union was checked by product identity: **9 core families + 50 landscape families − Bingers counted in both = 58**. It contains **49 active public presences, seven uncertain leads, and two discontinued services**. Active means current first-party material is available, not a successful signup, checkout, sync or alert test. No product was operationally exercised by this synthesis. The 29 detailed landscape paid-offer sections and eight core families with documented paid offers reconcile to **36 distinct detailed paid-offer families** after Bingers deduplication; this includes IMDb's professional offer and incomplete matrices. Separate B2B offers do not make an otherwise free consumer app paid.

This is a **bounded inventory**, not every app on the internet. Commercial-model labels have different meanings: **explicitly free** is a publisher policy; **no paid tier found** is incomplete evidence; **donation/supporter** can be voluntary or include a real entitlement; **paid offer with unknown matrix** proves monetization but not which advertised functions are paid. Prices remain literal: US Apple observations are not world prices; an unspecified `$` is not silently USD; a larger SKU is not automatically annual/lifetime. Identical repeated labels/amounts with no distinguishable terms are consolidated and noted. Different currencies, periods, platforms, named offers and eligibility qualifications remain separate. Expired promotions are marked historical, never the default current price.

### Material reconciliations

- **Bingers is active public presence, not a future launch.** Its [terms, updated October 14](https://bingers.app/terms), establish free-with-ads and Premium ad removal, resolving the landscape's ads uncertainty. Hero/Legend/Icon price-pair periods and mapping to Premium remain unknown. The original [TV Time homepage](https://www.tvtime.com/) is a farewell, not an active original-service paywall.
- **Serializd is not “no paid tier”.** Its [homepage](https://www.serializd.com/) and creator say tracker/stats/reminders are free, while [Patreon tiers](https://www.patreon.com/Serializd/membership) sell ad removal, badges and support/community rewards. Refract similarly makes [tracking free and Pro cosmetic](https://getrefract.app/); Suite TV [documents free functional core and Gold cosmetics/ad removal](https://www.suitetv.app/en/about).
- **Trakt cannot be assigned one certain current cap table.** [February structure](https://forums.trakt.tv/t/updating-trakt-limits-for-2026/101592), [March follow-up](https://forums.trakt.tv/t/follow-up-fair-use-abuse-prevention-what-happens-at-the-limits/102004), [July app-limit announcement](https://forums.trakt.tv/t/an-update-to-community-app-connections/117898), store “unlimited” text and server-supplied current limits differ in date/status. [October's update](https://roadmap.trakt.tv/changelog/rocket-trakt-product-update-october-2026) makes external calendars explicitly VIP and developer registration no longer VIP-gated. Old automated backups were [retired](https://forums.trakt.tv/t/retiring-automatic-backups/102430). September VIP previews remain previews.
- **Not every export or statistic is paid.** SIMKL's [backup page](https://simkl.com/apps/backup/) provides free CSV and manual JSON once/month; all-time stats are [free in its current plan page](https://simkl.com/vip/). Letterboxd [offers account export](https://letterboxd.com/api-beta/) without an established paid gate. Movie Paradise [documents free export once per 24 hours](https://movieparadise.app/press). These are baseline expectations for adoption, not paid-only marketing hooks.
- **Caps describe different objects.** Next Episode's [administrator clarification](https://forum.next-episode.net/viewtopic.php?pid=190046) says no show-count Watchlist cap, a 50-show Episodes view, and 25 movies in My Movies. [Sofa](https://www.sofahq.com/pricing) has unlimited lists but two concurrent tracked items free; [Sequel](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sequel-media-tracker/id1630746993) has unlimited tracked items but five collections free. Do not compress these into “limited library”.
- **Several current offers remain unresolved.** Taste's [store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/taste-movie-tv-suggestions/id1361180197) proves Premium prices but not its complete gates. Cinexplore's [publisher notes](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_SG&id=com.fidloo.cinexplore) prove one paid layout control, not that every advertised statistic/alert is paid. FilmNoir, Suite TV, Plex and Sofa Time price/gate conflicts are retained in the ledger rather than resolved by choosing the cheapest observation.

### Offer-to-capability schema and interpretation

The price ledger is also a bounded entitlement map. All **302 offer observations** now have plan-specific summaries, a platform-scoped group and explicit associations. The inventory remains **58 families**; the corrected opportunity ledger contains **347 rows**. A price observation, an entitlement and a GoodWatch implementation are different objects.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `offer_id` / `opportunity_id` | Stable row IDs. Existing OP-001–334 remain addressable; atomic splits and omissions were appended as OP-335–347. |
| `entitlement_group_id` | A documented tier/subset within its platform scope. Billing/currency variants may share this subset; this does not establish purchase transfer or complete platform parity. Higher support amounts can share identical benefits. |
| `entitlement_inherits_group_ids` | Explicit parent group IDs, directed from higher to lower. Only documented inheritance is represented; the graph must be acyclic. No automatic family-wide inheritance. |
| `entitlement_direct_opportunity_ids` | This group's explicitly mapped additions, not every capability advertised anywhere by the product. |
| `entitlement_opportunity_ids` | Materialized union of direct and inherited capability IDs. Source/row qualifications still apply; these are documentary entitlements, not live enforcement tests. Higher-tier numerical values in the capability row override lower-tier values. |
| `context_opportunity_ids` | Related free benchmarks, dated allocations, previews, disputed gates, exclusions or unknown SKU mappings. **These links do not grant the capability.** Empty entitlement IDs mean no positive allocation established, not “zero useful features.” |
| `feature_association_status`, `entitlement_sources`, `entitlement_scope_note` | State, primary support and limits of the allocation. `paid_matrix_unknown`, `tier_mapping_unknown`, dated/historical, free-baseline and support-only states remain distinct. |
| Opportunity `entitled_offer_ids` / `context_offer_ids` | Exact reverse associations, separately typed. A multi-product capability row does not transfer one competitor's benefits to another. |

The standalone offer viewer can filter documented entitlements versus context-only/unmapped observations and select an entitlement group. Click OP IDs to inspect capabilities, PLAN IDs to inspect offers, or EG IDs to inspect groups and inheritance. Search includes all fields. Full CSV downloads retain every record regardless of filters.

Representative safeguards: SIMKL VIP inherits the documented PRO subset with higher caps; Supporter only accelerates a conditional loyalty reward and has no PRO/VIP inheritance. Unproven per-control V2 gates remain contextual. Letterboxd Patron inherits Pro within platform groups, while Android icons remain unconfirmed. TVmaze Silver adds iCal/RSS to Bronze, and Gold adds support/recognition. Next Episode mobile does not inherit web Premium; Showly iOS does not inherit Android source-string benefits; Plex Remote Watch Pass does not inherit Plex Pass downloads/DVR. Criticker's one-time recalculation buys only that operation. Refract's named cosmetic SKU allocation and Bingers Hero/Legend/Icon→Premium mapping remain unknown. Free benchmarks, retired offers and donations never inherit all paid family features.

### Exact-control corrections and acceptance review

- **Sorting (OP-322–325, OP-335–341):** Compound rows are split into eleven controls. [My library](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768/goodwatch-webapp/app/domain/my-library.ts) and [My scores](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768/goodwatch-webapp/app/domain/my-scores.ts) implement personal-score sorting; [Wishlist ordering](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768/goodwatch-webapp/app/domain/watch-next.ts) implements Newest with movie-release/show-last-air semantics. No selectable progress, remaining-episode, next-airdate, DVD-date, episode-count, runtime, catalog-rank, drop-rate or vote-count sort was found across the inspected discovery, My movies, My shows, library and scores paths. Internal tie-breaks/fixed activity order are not those controls.
- **Hiding (OP-066/067/320, OP-342/343):** [Tracking actions](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768/goodwatch-webapp/app/ui/tracking/actions.ts) and [Hidden titles](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768/goodwatch-webapp/app/routes/my-library.hidden.tsx) provide narrower whole-title/recommendation hiding; no explicit episode, special or season hide preference was found. Excluding specials from completion and collapsing a season are different behaviors.
- **Exact acceptance:** Every opportunity's missing behavior, acceptance and dependencies were reviewed. Location history (OP-075) now requires optional occurrence-level location capture/edit/delete, private defaults and permission for device location. Prediction export (OP-150) requires generated score identifiers, model/time/scale and separation from own opinions; account export/restore is not its acceptance test. Other corrections cover biometric locks, prediction refresh, username changes, alternative airdates, queue order, outbound notification boundaries and native TV Time import. No live feature was promoted by this pass.
- **SIMKL additions (OP-344–346):** The [comparison](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/account-and-billing/subscription-plans/features-comparison) gates group selection and anime-title language to PRO/VIP. The [group guide](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/core-features/social-and-community/watch-party-with-friends) describes wanted/seen comparison with up to three friends and a share link, not synchronized playback. GoodWatch has no group chooser; [title-language settings](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768/goodwatch-webapp/app/routes/settings.language.tsx) partially overlap with English/Japanese localization, but no distinct Romaji choice was found. Supporter loyalty acceleration is explicitly a nonfunctional support mechanism; the [loyalty guide](https://docs.simkl.org/how-to-use-simkl/account-and-billing/special-plans/offers/loyalty-program) and PRO FAQ disagree on reward duration.
- **Next Episode (OP-347):** Website ad removal now has its own [Premium evidence](https://next-episode.net/premium) and offer association, without assigning it to the separate mobile purchases.

Source and issue findings are the October 19 audit snapshot; no new live verification was performed.

Use the [domain contract](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768/CONTEXT.md): Wishlist is intention; a Watch is an occurrence; a Pass is a run through a show; Seen and the five stored show states are not generic “completed” labels. A show rating does not set its stored status; movie rating can create an undated watch. Later episode releases do not silently change stored Seen. My library is not a diary or an ownership inventory. A taste match is not a personal predicted-score accuracy claim.

Current statuses remain separate from planning:

- **Implemented in code:** Connected implementation found; deployment unverified.
- **Partial:** Useful implementation, material missing behavior or fidelity.
- **Absent in inspected scope:** No relevant consumer implementation found in the scoped audit; not proof about every private branch.
- **Unknown:** Exact control, live setting, fidelity or competitor boundary cannot be established.
- **Concrete issue:** Linked existing scope; no delivery promise. **Related issue** means it does not authorize the proposed equivalent. **Uncommitted suggestion** means this report proposes work only. Professional/playback exclusions and supporter rewards receive explicit dispositions.

### Sustainable free policy proposal

Make **functional access free for ordinary personal use**, with a documented account requirement for durable/private state and transparent fair-use protection. This is a proposal, not current published policy. Do not promise unbounded AI, storage, delivery or third-party API work. Publish caps by their actual object (request, item, occurrence, byte, batch, concurrent job, calendar feed), reset behavior and what happens at the limit. Separate operational rate control from feature entitlement; do not secretly paywall exporting one's data or silently drop history.

Retain readable/exportable user data at a limit, offer resumable batches, expose queue/freshness status, deduplicate shared catalog work, cache repeated recommendations, and prefer digests/changed-item sync to constant full scans. Measure capacity before choosing new numeric limits. Current guest 20-rating and importer/MCP bounds should be disclosed, not renamed unlimited; this strategy does not require anonymous unlimited server storage. Evaluate abuse exceptions without converting routine features into paid gates. Optional donations/cosmetics can fund the service without exclusive core functions or destructive downgrade behavior.

Budget monitoring should cover per-import catalog lookups, storage growth per occurrence/review/image, aggregation refreshes, AI/reranking calls, provider scans, notification attempts, external API traffic, sync retries, feed polling and support/recovery load. No speculative engineering dollar estimate or ship date is supported by this research. Existing #688 budget intent is a constraint to verify, not proof every proposed capability is affordable.
