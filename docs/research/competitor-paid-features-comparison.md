# Paid features vs GoodWatch: research overview

**Research snapshot: 2026-10-19. Expanded overview: baseline, named-product and deep-discovery evidence integrated; bounded research, not an exhaustive census or a release.**

Start with paid-feature gaps, then inspect the exact vendor differences. This overview groups **121 product families**, **514 offer observations** and **757 capability records** so their free baselines, paid differences, exact limits and uncertainties can be compared without choosing a build order. These counts include adjacent services, historical offers and open evidence; they are not 514 current subscriptions or 757 distinct paid-only features.

The interactive report is an exploratory research tool. Product selection narrows capability records by overlap, while each record retains its full vendor and tier qualifications. GoodWatch status is prominent in the default paid-feature comparison, with code, partial behavior, concrete issues and unknowns kept separate. No independently verified live GoodWatch parity claim is established. No new roadmap, implementation priority or campaign is proposed by this design phase.

## Default: paid features versus GoodWatch

The default report now opens on **Gaps: 39 Missing + 10 Partial paid capabilities**. Every compact row shows the capability, paid competitors/tiers, a prominent GoodWatch state, and a one-line gap. **Have (8)**, **Not checked (24)**, **Missing**, **Partial**, and **All paid (81)** are one-click filters. Category, competitor and text filters narrow the evidence; GoodWatch’s curated state stays scoped to the whole named capability rather than turning green for a favorable selected variant. Six cosmetic groups are optional and off by default. The 81 functional capabilities span nine jobs; appearance is a tenth optional group.

This is a presentation layer over the unchanged three CSVs and five source dossiers. **352 source records** are explicitly mapped to **87 normalized capabilities**; **405 records** remain outside the compact view. **252 offer observations** pass the offer gate, before per-record exclusions. These are not counts of subscriptions or distinct vendors. Generated prediction export (OP-150) is its own Missing capability: model-generated scores, model/time/scale/uncertainty and stable title IDs are distinct from exporting user ratings or account data. Tier chips prefer a normal offer label within the same documented entitlement group where available among eligible observations; no price sorting is used, and gift/store observations stay intact in details. No priorities, build order or new research are introduced.

### Eligibility and conservative status rules

The [view metadata](competitor-paid-features-view.json) records every feature mapping, all 514 offer decisions, all 757 source-record decisions, reasons, source hashes and explicit status rationale. It is embedded once as the **tenth companion**, alongside the original nine. An expandable eligibility audit in Evidence supports record/reason search; the complete JSON has view/copy/download fallback.

- **Included:** Exact documented entitlement links to consumer or functional supporter offers. A price may be unknown when the pay gate is documented. Capacity increases remain real paid differences; the free core is not relabeled paid. Store eligibility/price observations stay qualified in drill-down.
- **Excluded:** Context-only/IAP-only evidence, free-only benchmarks, discontinued-only offers, trial-only or unsettled historical price observations, configuration-only prices, future/beta gates, professional/B2B and licensed playback/server operations, human supporter perks. Other qualified offers can still establish the same feature. Three specialized controls—OP-249 quick-rating prompt, OP-321 language hiding, OP-515 Telegram integration—remain explicitly secondary; their scope is not silently merged into unrelated capabilities.
- **Functional supporter exceptions:** Criticker/SIMKL, Annict and MyEpisodes functional paid gates remain eligible. Anime-Planet Bronze+ ad removal is included; Tip Jar human recognition does not grant it. MyEpisodes patron feed/under-development badge stays out.
- **Platform and source exceptions:** WatchNext Premium/trial do not gain Unlimited calendar/export tools. Stremio household/PIN controls remain consumer software; downloaded video, skipping and subtitle/player operations stay out. Reeel/Showly iOS unallocated purchases do not inherit backend/Android entitlements. CineTrak regional ad-removal evidence and Filmweb/Filmarks ad exceptions remain visible; no universal ad-free claim follows.
- **Have:** Every included source variant is code-backed for the narrowly named capability. No Have state for unlimited capacity, eight statuses, global diary, arbitrary past-time entry, native review writing, wearable controls or automatic capture. All deployment remains unverified.
- **Partial:** A meaningful scoped subset exists and the short explanation names the gap. Calendar-day backdating is Have; arbitrary historical time is Partial. Internal calendars and external iCal, taste statistics and watch statistics, file import and sync remain separate.
- **Not checked:** Exact support is unassessed or variants cannot support a whole-feature conclusion. Unknown never becomes Missing. Legacy tracking/template or share-card overlaps are conservatively Not checked for exact capacity/artwork/icon controls; original source classifications remain visible in drill-down rather than being rewritten.

Competitors and Evidence are secondary tabs. They preserve all 121 families, eleven-name reconciliation, 19 research leads, 514 offers, 757 raw capabilities, inherited/reverse offer links and source snapshots. Product and feature navigation respects the active view and browser history. The dense paid comparison replaces the former directory-first initial screen; no research data is discarded.

### Offer-to-capability schema and interpretation

The price ledger is also a bounded entitlement map. All **514 offer observations** have plan-specific summaries, a platform-scoped group and explicit associations. The inventory contains **121 families** and the capability ledger **757 records**. The original 58 family IDs, PLAN-001–302 and OP-001–347 remain addressable. New observations append PLAN-303–514 and OP-348–743; exact-control splits append OP-744–757; new rows have no proposed priority or wave. A price observation, an entitlement and a GoodWatch implementation are different objects.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `offer_id` / `opportunity_id` | Stable row IDs. Existing OP-001–334 remain addressable; atomic splits and omissions were appended as OP-335–347. |
| `entitlement_group_id` | A documented tier/subset within its platform scope. Billing/currency variants may share this subset; this does not establish purchase transfer or complete platform parity. Higher support amounts can share identical benefits. |
| `entitlement_inherits_group_ids` | Explicit parent group IDs, directed from higher to lower. Only documented inheritance is represented; the graph must be acyclic. No automatic family-wide inheritance. |
| `entitlement_direct_opportunity_ids` | This group's explicitly mapped additions, not every capability advertised anywhere by the product. |
| `entitlement_opportunity_ids` | Materialized union of direct and inherited capability IDs. Source/row qualifications still apply; these are documentary entitlements, not live enforcement tests. Higher-tier numerical values in the capability row override lower-tier values. |
| `context_opportunity_ids` | Related free benchmarks, dated allocations, previews, disputed gates, exclusions or unknown SKU mappings. **These links do not grant the capability.** Empty entitlement IDs mean no positive allocation established, not “zero useful features.” |
| `feature_association_status`, `entitlement_sources`, `entitlement_scope_note` | State, primary support and limits of the allocation. `paid_matrix_unknown`, `tier_mapping_unknown`, dated/historical, free-baseline and support-only states remain distinct. |
| Opportunity `entitled_offer_ids` / `context_offer_ids` | Exact reverse associations, separately typed. A multi-product capability row does not transfer one competitor's benefits to another. |

The standalone offer viewer can filter documented entitlements versus context-only/unmapped observations and select an entitlement group. Click OP IDs to inspect capabilities, PLAN IDs to inspect offers, or EG IDs to inspect groups and inheritance. Search includes all fields. Complete source and copy/download attempts retain every record regardless of filters. Downloads work on the isolated HTTP test route but may be blocked by the hosting viewer.

Representative safeguards: SIMKL VIP inherits the documented PRO subset with higher caps; Supporter only accelerates a conditional loyalty reward and has no PRO/VIP inheritance. Unproven per-control V2 gates remain contextual. Letterboxd Patron inherits Pro within platform groups, while Android icons remain unconfirmed. TVmaze Silver adds iCal/RSS to Bronze, and Gold adds support/recognition. Next Episode mobile does not inherit web Premium; Showly iOS does not inherit Android source-string benefits; Plex Remote Watch Pass does not inherit Plex Pass downloads/DVR. Criticker's one-time recalculation buys only that operation. Refract's named cosmetic SKU allocation and Bingers Hero/Legend/Icon→Premium mapping remain unknown. Free benchmarks, retired offers and donations never inherit all paid family features.

## GoodWatch snapshot and interpretation

The GoodWatch audit inspected checkout HEAD [`518cc8210cade63e00e173c07d0523dd09e7067e`](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/commit/518cc8210cade63e00e173c07d0523dd09e7067e) on `feat/shared-scoring`, and independently inspected the then-current main object [`b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768`](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/commit/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768). Source and issue findings are the October 19 audit snapshot; no new live verification was performed.

Use the [domain contract](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/blob/b15b6cbe6f8dd8aa4fc861b5e7c8443c37d39768/CONTEXT.md): Wishlist is intention; a Watch is an occurrence; a Pass is a run through a show; Seen and the five stored show states are not generic “completed” labels. A show rating does not set its stored status; movie rating can create an undated watch. Later episode releases do not silently change stored Seen. My library is not a diary or an ownership inventory. ### Sustainable free policy proposal

Make **functional access free for ordinary personal use**, with a documented account requirement for durable/private state and transparent fair-use protection. This is a proposal, not current published policy. Do not promise unbounded AI, storage, delivery or third-party API work. Publish caps by their actual object (request, item, occurrence, byte, batch, concurrent job, calendar feed), reset behavior and what happens at the limit. Separate operational rate control from feature entitlement; do not secretly paywall exporting one's data or silently drop history.

Retain readable/exportable user data at a limit, offer resumable batches, expose queue/freshness status, deduplicate shared catalog work, cache repeated recommendations, and prefer digests/changed-item sync to constant full scans. Measure capacity before choosing new numeric limits. Current guest 20-rating and importer/MCP bounds should be disclosed, not renamed unlimited; this strategy does not require anonymous unlimited server storage. Evaluate abuse exceptions without converting routine features into paid gates. Optional donations/cosmetics can fund the service without exclusive core functions or destructive downgrade behavior.

Budget monitoring should cover per-import catalog lookups, storage growth per occurrence/review/image, aggregation refreshes, AI/reranking calls, provider scans, notification attempts, external API traffic, sync retries, feed polling and support/recovery load. No speculative engineering dollar estimate or ship date is supported by this research. Existing #688 budget intent is a constraint to verify, not proof every proposed capability is affordable.
