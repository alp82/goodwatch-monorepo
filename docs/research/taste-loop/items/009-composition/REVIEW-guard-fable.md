# Review of the T2a disliked-share guard by Fable (read-only), October 14, 2026

Saved by the orchestrator from the reviewer's final message. Written before item 009's results were read.

**Verdict: the disliked-share guard is degenerate against B2, but changing it now does not rescue F1 or F3 (both still fail the animation guard), so there is no reason to decide it under pressure. Fix the guard as a protocol amendment before G1's results are read, build the T2a gate, and judge G1 under the amended rule. Register nothing from item 007.**

1. **Defective by construction, with one real thing underneath.**
   - The guard is a recall of disliked titles: "share of hidden titles rated 5 or less that reach the first 50" (PLAN.md:108-109, PROTOCOL.md:257-260). Any scorer that surfaces titles the member actually watched raises it, because about 20% of what members watch they dislike (973 of 4,788 hidden titles, ladder-T2a.md:13). B2's value of 0.001 is a floor: it surfaces almost nothing watched (loved 0.019, ladder-T2a.md:55), and its owner list is obscure titles with scores down to 37 (DECISIONS.md:36). Against a reference at the floor, "not worse with an interval excluding zero" is unpassable for any scorer that puts watched titles at the top. B5 passes it for the same reason B2 does (0.001/0.001, ladder-T2a.md:58), with a top of stand-up specials (DECISIONS.md:27).
   - The loop already wrote this down before item 007 and then applied it one-sidedly: "first-50 shares favour popular scorers for every band" (DECISIONS.md:91-93) was used to discount B3's loved-share gain, while the same band's disliked share was counted as a failed guard (DECISIONS.md:16). It cannot be both.
   - My own check on the ledger aggregates (title-weighted, approximate; the harness averages per member): implied disliked titles among watched titles reaching the first 50 are B2 ~1 of 49 (2%), B3 ~24 of 252 (10%), F1 ~25 of 263 (9%), F3 ~24 of 240 (10%), vote count ~306 of 1,429 (21%), against a base rate of 28% among loved-plus-disliked hidden titles. The candidates halve the dislike rate of popularity alone and of the members' own history. B2's 2% rests on one title.
   - Already in the tables, and the cleanest evidence: among equally popular candidates, disliked titles land *lower* under F1 and F3 than under B2: −0.069 (−0.098 to −0.041) and −0.063 (−0.093 to −0.034) (RESULTS.md:121, 189). So the absolute guard is a popularity artefact, not dislike-surfacing.
   - What is real: the candidates lean popular (median votes 111,605 against 7,049, RESULTS.md:132) and lift *all* watched titles, disliked ones included (F1 pct_low +0.030, +0.003 to +0.059, RESULTS.md:115). That is the popularity tilt, already caught by the vote-count guard and by the matched measures. The disliked-share guard double-counts it.

2. **Defensible replacement: use the popularity-matched disliked percentile, which already exists and predates item 007.**
   - Definition: per member with 5 or more titles rated 5 or less (26 members), the mean percentile of those titles among candidates within a factor of 1.5 in votes (`pct_popular_low`, PROTOCOL.md:273-277, item 004). Paired against the primary reference B2, 10,000 draws, registered seed. Fails if higher with a 95% interval excluding zero. Same form as the other guards, applied to every rung.
   - Why this and not the options offered: (a) the precision ratio is the right idea but count-poor: ~4 hits per member in 50 slots, undefined for B2 for most members, and a guard with a wide interval is a guard that is easy to pass. Report it (per member, minimum 5 hidden titles in the first 50, against the member's own share of 5-or-less among hidden titles), do not gate on it. (b) B3 as the guard reference while B2 is the primary reference mixes references; B3 is not established over B2 (separation −0.004, ladder-T2a.md:32), so it cannot stand in. (c) Keeping the rule makes T2a unable to pass any scorer that surfaces watched titles; that is enshrining B2, not caution. T2a's own text says it cannot see whether unrated titles above a favourite are any good (PLAN.md:116-117); the disliked share was a weak proxy for T3, and should be marked as such.
   - Keep the absolute shares in every table as descriptive; nothing is deleted.
   - One more inconsistency to fix in the same amendment: "median vote count" has no stated direction yet was listed under failed guards (DECISIONS.md:16). Either give it a bound or make it descriptive.

3. **What makes the change acceptable, and what would not.**
   - Acceptable: a dated PROTOCOL.md amendment, reviewed by two families as PLAN.md:397 requires, stating that it was made after tier A results of items 003-007 and listing retroactively which verdicts it changes (B3, C3, F1, F3 pass this guard; none becomes a win, see 4). The rule applies to every rung and every candidate, past and future. The old guard stays reported. The amendment is committed **before** the orchestrator reads item 009's RESULTS.md (the brief withheld PREREG for exactly this, 009/BRIEF.md:34-35). The gate look is the real safeguard, and it is not built: `gate.py` refuses any task but T1 (lab/gate.py:70, PROTOCOL.md:325-326), though PLAN.md:181 says T2a uses the gate. Item 008 just showed a tier A win failing to replicate (DECISIONS.md:114); registering a T2a candidate with no gate would repeat the first loop's spent-locked-set failure.
   - Unacceptable: picking among guard forms by which one F1 passes; changing the primary, the 0.03 floor, or the animation, shows and franchise guards; using B3 as reference for guards only; applying the new rule only to item 007 candidates; registering before the gate supports T2a; reading G1's results first and amending after; counting the owner's "looks plausible" (DECISIONS.md:28-30) as evidence.

4. **What to do for D1.**
   - Do not register F1 or F3. Even with the disliked guard replaced, both fail animation against the members' own mix with intervals excluding zero (F1 +0.165, +0.131 to +0.200; F3 +0.162, +0.128 to +0.197, RESULTS.md:128, 196), and that guard is not in question. F1 also misses item 007's own fixed franchise limit (NO-PREREG.md:7). The guard change is therefore not a rescue of anything already seen, which is the strongest argument that it can be made honestly now.
   - Order: (1) commit the protocol amendment of answer 2 and its two reviews; (2) extend `gate.py` to T2a and add a test, as item 006 did for other fixes; (3) only then open 009's RESULTS.md. If G1 meets the amended rule on tier A (separation against B2 with interval excluding zero and at least 0.03; shows, animation, franchise, matched disliked percentile none worse with an interval excluding zero), write its PREREG, run the registered run, take one gate look. Not replicated means null for D1 offline and recruiting for T3 waits.
   - If G1 fails too, D1 stays "keep B2" and the next brief should state the popularity tilt as the open question, not the disliked share.
