## Implementation and interpretation

`lab/candidates/009/g1_mix.py` copies item 007’s F3 unchanged apart from renaming its entry point, then greedily fills the first 100 positions. For each share independently, a proposed title must give a share within an inclusive 10 percentage points of the visible rated-title share, or, starting at position 11, strictly closer than placing the opposite kind next. Both shares must qualify. If none qualifies, the next unplaced F3 title is taken. Stable view order breaks F3 ties. Remaining unplaced titles keep F3 order after position 100. The returned ranks are unique ordinals over the final list, including the suffix; F3 ties therefore use their stable view order rather than tied midranks. B5’s score and p_low are returned unchanged. No ratings means no defined own mix, so F3 is retained.

The candidate’s baseline uses **all visible rated titles**, as specified. T2a guards use the member’s rated universe titles (including hidden ratings in the evaluator); T2b guards use rated **and added** universe titles before the cutoff. Guards also exclude hidden/target titles and T2a side-list titles from the first 50. The running constraint therefore does not impose a hard ±10-point bound on the evaluator’s first-50 guard. Fallback can violate it too. No franchise or vote rule was added to G1.

F3 cannot be a reference: `run.py` requires every reference to be an unchanged pinned rung under `lab/ladder/`. The brief’s B2/B3/B5 fallback was used without spending a rejected invocation. F3 figures below are directly from item 007’s ledger lines. Historical F3 differences are descriptive differences of rounded aggregate estimates; no paired G1-versus-F3 interval was computed.

## Reading the figures

All intervals are 95%, using 10,000 draws and seed 20261013. Others are equally weighted members, with paired candidate-minus-reference differences and member bootstrap intervals. Higher separation, target percentile and loved-title retrieval are better; lower disliked retrieval and later-skip percentile are better. Shares are fractions. `first50_high` is the share of hidden loved titles reaching the first 50, not the share of the top 50 that is loved.

T2a has 61 other members for loved-title retrieval/composition and 26 with both five-title rating bands for separation. Owner T2a sitting intervals are unavailable because one sitting holds 34% of the separation titles. Owner franchise intervals are sensitivity checks, not uncertainty about future ratings. T2b has only 3 other members for sitting and 9 for order; owner T2b intervals resample add sittings. Smallest detectable effects (SDE) are the protocol’s planning approximation of 2.80 standard errors. Missing intervals are not zero-width intervals. The test concerns hidden previously watched titles; matching on public score or votes reduces confounds without proving personal recommendation quality.

## Guard definitions and unavailable measures

`top_shows_vs_own` and `top_animation_vs_own` are **signed** differences, not absolute distances. A negative paired difference on either is not automatically an improvement: it could increase an existing deficit. Composition conclusions must compare distance to the member’s own mix, rather than treat larger or smaller shares as universally better. The harness supplies no paired interval for absolute mix distance. The orchestrator reviews these definitions before deciding registration.

`top_franchise_max` is each view’s largest movie-series count in its first 50, averaged per member; other titles are singleton franchises. `top_votes_median` is the mean across members of view-level median vote counts. T2a `first50_low` and `first200_low` measure disliked-title retrieval. `matched` compares within 3 GoodWatch score points; `popular` within a factor of 1.5 in votes; `both` combines 3 score points with a factor of 2 and requires 50 matched candidates. Retention counts and eligible samples are listed above.

T2b `later_pct` pools later Skip and Not interested; `auc` is Want to See versus those pooled rows, a stand-in rather than a judged negative outcome. Separate Skip/Not interested measures are not provided by the harness. Touched-first-50 and touched-listed counts must both be zero. B5 score errors are reported for T2a; B2/B3 have no T2a score.
