# Core movie and TV competitors: paid-feature evidence dossier

Research/access date: **2026-10-19**. Scope: IMDb/IMDbPro, JustWatch, Trakt, SIMKL, Letterboxd, TV Time, Serializd, Criticker, and the material TV Time successor Bingers. This is a competitor evidence inventory, not an audit of GoodWatch functionality or an approved advertising claim.

## How to use this evidence

The tables separate a documented feature from its price, limits, platform and delivery status. **Documented** means a first-party page was read; it does not mean the feature was exercised. No accounts, trials, purchases, paid content or authenticated APIs were used. Search results were leads; claims below use opened primary pages or explicitly identified anonymous first-party HTML/source inspection. The existing market baseline was read as a lead, not accepted as current evidence.

All source IDs link to exact URLs. The source register records supplied dates and access limitations; **every source was accessed on 2026-10-19**, including undated pages. A crawl date, relative search-engine publication estimate, copyright year, or this research date is not a publisher update date. An undated page is described as undated even when freshly accessible. US Apple listings are US storefront/USD observations, not worldwide prices. Web prices without a stated country are labeled accordingly. Tax and checkout totals were not inferred.

Status vocabulary:

- **Active/documented:** A present product, plan or feature is offered by an accessible primary source.
- **Announced:** The publisher describes an intention or rollout; current enforcement/delivery is not established.
- **Beta/preview:** Explicitly experimental or early access; do not promise stable equivalence.
- **Discontinued:** Retirement/farewell evidence; retain historical context without advertising it as a current paid competitor.
- **Unknown:** Not publicly documented, inaccessible, ambiguous, or dependent on authentication. This never means zero, absent, free, or unlimited.
- **Conflict:** Sources disagree; both observations are preserved. Neither becomes an unqualified campaign claim.

Feature names in competitor tables preserve competitor terminology. A competitor “watchlist,” “library,” or “collection” is not automatically the same object as GoodWatch's Wishlist or My library.

## Recommendations for the later strategy thread

These are recommendations from the evidence, **not factual claims about GoodWatch's implementation**.

1. **Build a behavior-and-limit claim ledger before writing parity copy.** Every claim needs a competitor, plan, date, platform, free baseline, behavior, cap and reproducible verification. “Stats,” “lists” and “sync” are too broad to prove equivalence.
2. **Start candidate comparisons with the clearest gates:** JustWatch runtime/vote/production-country filters, hiding seen/disliked titles and extra lists; Letterboxd annual/all-time/list analytics and availability alerts; SIMKL paid privacy/custom-list scale/iCal/automation; Criticker richer taste statistics and daily prediction freshness. The detailed tables, not this prioritization, are the evidence.
3. **Preserve free competitor strengths.** IMDb already has large free list caps and CSV exports. SIMKL has free all-time stats and backup alternatives. Serializd advertises free tracking/stats/notifications and sells support/ad removal. Calling these paid-only would undermine verified-parity positioning.
4. **Exclude industry-only services and licensed playback from a generic utility promise.** IMDbPro contacts/page management, organizational HQ services and rental/streaming rights require separate decisions.
5. **Treat Trakt as a live research risk.** Do not turn February limits, current source constants, old VIP pages or “unlimited” store text into a single certain current offer. Its current authenticated usage and checkout are missing evidence, not blanks to fill with guesses.
6. **Make migration fidelity a comparison dimension.** Check watched events, unknown dates, ratings, reviews, statuses, custom order, private notes, episode numbering and repeated same-day watches separately. An import button does not prove preservation.
7. **Track successor changes.** Bingers has launched and has paid store entries. Original TV Time is no longer an active paid target. Trakt's similarly named fork and unrelated “TV Time” store apps require publisher-level identity checks.
8. **Use narrower claims until gaps close.** A dated claim about a specific verified feature is supportable in principle; “every useful paid feature” remains an internal research objective, not a finding of this dossier.
