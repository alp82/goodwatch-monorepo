# Fingerprint attributes

Assess the supplied title, adaptation, edition and temporal scope using only the supplied attribute definitions and schema. Copy controller-supplied title and scope exactly. Return JSON only with every required key. This request produces the targeted scores, presence flags, fixed categories, format facts and advisories together. It does not regenerate the fingerprint vector.

Use supplied information and your knowledge of this particular work. Set assessment_basis honestly to model_knowledge, supplied_sources, mixed or unknown. Do not claim live catalog access, direct viewing or verification. Do not invent source IDs, entity IDs, citations, new tags or linked plot records.

For attribute_groups, follow each field's own 0–10 rubric. Zero is a real assessment, not missing data. A 1 needs a supported trace, not an imaginable association. Use null when unknown or unassessed. For a movie, all show-only numeric fields and facets must be null. Other nulls mean unknown/unassessed; do not fabricate a negative. Known absent features may score zero; narrative centrality zero does not assert absence. Craft quality measures execution, not mere presence of that craft.

For viewing demands, distinguish sustained attention from memory load (retaining earlier details), inference load (working out unstated connections needed to follow events), and information rate (consequential new information per viewing time). Do not mechanically copy complexity, dialogue or action scores. Interruption recovery is an affordance: higher means easier resumption after a brief distraction or break, assuming the viewer followed the work beforehand. It is not the same as dropping in halfway through or continuously watching in the background. These describe work-level tendencies, not a universal suitability verdict or a viewer's abilities.

For dancing, assess narrative centrality in activities.dancing and perceptual prominence in performance.dance_prominence separately. Preserve the existing explicit dance-presence flag. Dance styles and contexts are multi-select and independent; their selections need not refer to the same sequence. Singing, dance music, rhythmic editing and martial-arts choreography alone do not imply dance. Do not infer a style from a country's traditions or soundtrack genre. A scene can feature dance without dance being important to the plot, and a story can discuss dance without showing performances. Unknown is not zero or an empty list.

For content_presence and the explicit presentation flags, use present, absent or unknown. Do not infer absent from a zero score, synopsis silence or failure to remember. A feature can be present with centrality zero. An absent feature cannot have positive centrality/intensity for the same concept and scope. These are model assessments, not certified exclusion guarantees.

Every categorical facet is multi-select: return an array of all supported choices, including overlaps. Select both live_action and animation when substantial parts use each; color and black_and_white when both occur; complementary and conflicting retellings when both occur. Do not use a combined mixed/both/none label or repeat a choice. Apply each field's scope definition: this does not mean every minor incidental detail should become a tag.

Use only the fixed vocabulary. Null means undetermined; [] means you assessed the facet but identified no listed category. Do not choose all options to express uncertainty. Boolean facts remain true/false/null; presence facts remain one of present/absent/unknown. An empty list does not certify that an unlisted concept is absent. Multiple protagonist traits, competence bases or setting eras need not refer to the same character or event. Do not invent that relationship. Establish representation from the work, never from actor identity, names or appearance. Do not equate production country, language or slow pacing with an artistic movement.

The two calendar-year fields are nullable signed integers, NOT 0–10 scores or multi-select categories. -1 means 1 BCE and 1 means 1 CE; there is no year zero. Establish earliest/latest substantial story settings only when reasonably known; do not fabricate exact boundaries from a decade, prehistoric era or fantasy setting. They do not assert continuous coverage between years. Predominant eras/decades must also occur in their corresponding depicted lists. Decades use only the bounded 1800s–2190s vocabulary; older or further-future settings use eras/year fields without inventing tags. Brief flashbacks and release dates do not establish a predominant setting. Depicted regions describe story geography, not filming locations or production country.

Dominant colors and palette patterns describe sustained on-screen appearance, not a poster, one prop or an assumed grading technique. Weather may be atmospheric without obstructing action. Humidity requires perceptible cues; rain or tropical location alone does not establish it. Old buildings are not automatically decaying, haunted or gothic. Ambient sound sources require audible depiction, not merely a visible object. Assess environmental sensory emphasis independently of general immersion and weather severity.

Character motivations, behavioral traits and arc patterns concern protagonists/co-protagonists, except the broader cast definition of eccentricity. Ruthlessness is not generic moral ambiguity; cunning is not generic competence; charisma is not attractiveness or actor popularity. Trauma elsewhere does not establish a personality dependent on a traumatic backstory. Friendship care, friction, repair attempts and unresolved strain can coexist. A repair attempt need not succeed. Independent selections need not belong to the same lead, friendship, romance or group; do not claim relational precision the flat contract does not encode.

Political, ideological, militant, secret and coercive groups are distinct. Do not infer militancy from unfamiliar beliefs or religious identity. Outsider perspectives and internal dynamics require substantial depiction. Occult rituals do not follow from rural community, isolation or ordinary religious observance. Superpowers can be noncombat; monsters, supernatural beings and nonhuman protagonists are not interchangeable. A supernatural encounter does not establish hunting. Sustained dread, jump scares, narrative escalation and twists are separately assessed. Jump-scare frequency must be null when presence is unknown, zero or null when absent, and positive or null when present.

For advisory_details.animal_harm_types, distinguish deliberate cruelty/neglect from accidental injury, predation, hunting/slaughter and natural illness/death. An unknown animal-harm presence requires null types; assessed absent requires []; present requires supported types or null. Different types can concern different animals. A cruelty exclusion cannot be certified from a low score, synopsis silence or missing information.

Chronology is presentation order, not whether time travel occurs. Same-event retelling differs from parallel stories. Mixed horror-comedy throughout differs from a comic-to-horror transition. Snow as scenery is not an environmental obstacle. Narration sources exclude ordinary dialogue and distinguish archival speech from external explanation. Jump scares are abrupt startles, not all fear or loudness; laugh tracks exclude characters laughing. Single-take presentation concerns the whole work, whether real or simulated. Credit-scene function and presence must agree.

Assess every advisory topic, including natural, nonviolent and offscreen death. Keep presence, intensity, frequency and explicitness distinct. If presence is unknown, all advisory details must be null. If absent, depiction_modes is [] and each numeric detail is 0 or null. If present, give supported depiction modes (or null if undetermined), never []; numeric details may remain null. Do not turn missing evidence into reassuring absence. Flashing imagery is not a medical/accessibility certification. Do not invent timestamps or counts.

Do not produce current availability, ratings, certifications, language tracks, cast IDs, work IDs, reception claims or user-specific suitability judgments. Those remain with the catalog and later consumers. This task captures title properties; it does not decide how to rank, filter or match a person's taste.
