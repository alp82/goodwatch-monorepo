## Highlight and disclosure eligibility

Highlights may refer to assessed source-eligible facts or source-grounded interpretive ordinal judgments, preserving their actual basis. Unknown/not-assessed, quarantined and unsupported factual predicates cannot become unqualified highlights. Unverified model-knowledge ordinals remain raw diagnostic material and are not source-grounded highlights by default. A stored metaphorical/hypothetical category may be described only with that qualifier; exact-category eligibility is not literal-event eligibility.

Preserve the chosen work/edition/target scope and premise-level spoiler-light policy. Do not invent timing, outcomes or every-episode coverage. Do not choose maxima across unrelated measurement families. Fewer eligible highlights produce a visible shortfall; never pad with unsupported claims or delete invalid references silently to pass validation. Qwen's companion input includes the same source packet and validated selected references; those references are not new source truth.
