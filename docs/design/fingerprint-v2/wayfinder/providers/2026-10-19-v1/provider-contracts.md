### Mapping the proposed common assessments

Preserving all 74 legacy traits at exact eleven-category precision is a **document compatibility constraint**, not a fair universal test of the best native classifier representation. It can disadvantage a coarser or probabilistic classifier. Give compared arms equivalent semantic definitions/anchors, preserve probabilities separately from magnitudes, and interpret adapter failure within this contract. A deliberate native coarse-Score experiment with an explicit legacy projection would answer another question; no rounding, mapping, new arm or scale change is approved here. Disclose this limitation before the pending Q8/Q9 protocol choices.

| Required result | Feasible finite encoding, derived from the API | Failure boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 74 integer 0–10 traits with explicit states | One Choice per trait: eleven independently described value options plus unknown, not applicable, not assessed (14 options) | Native Score allows at most 10 levels and returns a weighted fractional position; scaling/rounding would change the required semantics |
| Nine native 0–4 ordinals | Choice with five anchored assessed values and three states; optionally a separate declared Score experiment, not this adapter | Never put unknown/N/A on an ordinal magnitude ladder; don't replace the requested categorical ordinal with a mean |
| Binary presence or polarity | Choice for supported present, supported absent, unknown/N/A; distinguish asserted/averted/subverted/hypothetical where relevant | A Noul probability is not a measured intensity or proof of supported absence |
| Violence peak/exposure and color | Separate finite choices with explicit state and work/edition scope | No combined “violent” answer or blanket absence from omission |
| Six concepts, two scoped relations | Predeclare a finite candidate bank or complete assertion tuples, including no-supported-candidate/unknown; deterministic assembly chooses supported items | Cannot discover arbitrary new strings/participants or create missing evidence in this classifier call |
| Evidence attribution | Enumerate source/span IDs plus unsupported/conflicting/none states, or choose complete value/evidence/scope/polarity tuples | Separate questions cannot see one another's chosen value/evidence; mismatches need validation and must remain failures/unknown, not silent semantic repair |
| Free-text rationale, essence/tags/highlights, advisories and contexts | G produces the full required companion families from the same packet plus X outputs in the second request, with ownership and conflicts retained | A classifier alone is not a complete title analysis; G cannot silently overwrite X-owned fields |

[Choice](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/choice) permits at most **255 options** and returns selected key/distribution/confidence. Descriptions and instructions must explicitly name the trait: the question ID itself is not model-visible. [Score](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/score) is a probability-weighted value across ordered descriptions, not eleven discrete bins. [Structured descriptions](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/advanced) allow objects/arrays inside criteria; this does not make the output a generated arbitrary JSON record. [Confidence](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence) summarizes distribution shape; it is separate from trait strength and from validated factual support.

Derived sizing: the illustrative atomic question inventory is `74 + 9 + 2 + 1 + 6 + 2 + 4 = 98`, before separate evidence, scope, coverage or reason questions. Variable-sized candidate banks and multilabel support can require more. This count assumes one atomic question per listed assessment/slot; it is not a mathematical lower bound across all possible joint encodings. A packed alternative must still satisfy the semantic and finite-option contract, rather than hide multiple tasks in an untested choice. This is an inventory formula, **not a rendered request or token estimate**. If a joint Choice enumerates value × evidence × scope × polarity, verify its product ≤255 for every question; independently add nonassessed states. For example, 11 values × 24 evidence choices already exceeds 255 before scope/states. Do not evade limits by deleting attribution or hiding chained requests. Speculative parallel questions may stay in one call, with deterministic consistency checks, but their semantic compatibility remains untested.

All questions evaluate the same supplied state independently. The [parallel-question cookbook](https://docs.typesafe.ai/cookbooks/parallel_questions) shows state-sharing cost savings, but its example is an older regulatory task, not this schema or a current title result. State once plus every instruction/criterion/option is the planning model; **the exact tokenizer, fixed serialization overhead and billing mapping were not established**. Returned input/output usage is documented by the [HTTP API](https://docs.typesafe.ai/api); no preflight tokenize endpoint or request-level billed-input cap was found in the published surface. Do not assert that counting Qwen tokens proves Jev's 16k billed bound.

The vendor's [1.13 jaggedness](https://docs.typesafe.ai/model-jaggedness/jev-1.13) warns about literal negation/indirection, irrelevant long state, adversarial text, option order, numeric precision and inability to generate. Those are relevant negative findings for relations, exclusions and craft scoring. The page is explicitly for 1.13; neither its weaknesses nor their repair are measured for 1.14 here. No extra option-permutation calls are authorized by this research.

## Conservative billing and admission formulas

Use the approved envelope exactly: at most three configurations ×12 slots, two initial calls/slot, **72 initial +18 pooled retries**, title/prose ceiling **$3.50** inside the shared **$5**. Input ≤16,000 including schema/repair context and classifier billing units; total billed generation output **including reasoning** ≤8192. Concurrency 2, one logical pipeline at a time, attempt timeout 120 seconds, retry wait ≤60 seconds. This is a planning constraint, not execution approval. Other allocations stay reserved to their stages; the $0.40 contingency funds no optional work.

For a generative attempt r, in USD/M tokens:

```
C_r = (I_uncached*p_in + I_read*p_read + I_write*p_write
       + O_billed*p_out)/1e6 + F_request + F_storage + F_other
O_billed = visible completion + billed reasoning (avoid double counting)
R_r(cold) = (I_bound*p_in + 8192*p_out)/1e6 + known extra fees
```

If cache-write pricing could be additive to ordinary input, use both charges until its contract is resolved; otherwise partition tokens according to verified accounting. For a cold design with caching explicitly unused, use `I_bound ≤16000` including provider-added schema/template overhead; do not count only visible user text. Cache hits have a separate observed measure, never an assumed percentage. Account for BYOK upstream charges if applicable, credit-purchase fees/taxes separately from inference, and paid tools/response middleware if enabled. No purchase, BYOK or policy setting was inspected/changed. No web tools, search grounding, response healing, transforms or router model should be enabled in these identity candidates.

[OpenRouter reasoning documentation](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/best-practices/reasoning-tokens) says reasoning is billed even when hidden and generally shares `max_tokens`; its wording is “most providers,” not proof for every route. Accept G's disabled-thinking/cap behavior only after the scheduled compatibility response and usage checks, and reject any route without a reliable pre-dispatch bound. Reasoning families must use their actual supported effort and budget accounting. [Structured output support](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/structured-outputs) is route-specific; require advertised parameters and validate the full raw response locally, including coverage and finite IDs.

Pure token-exposure **illustrations**, conditional on verified 16k input/8192 billed output, no paid extras and current rates: G cold ≤`(16000*0.15+8192*0.47)/1e6 = $0.00625024` per attempt; its 3.7 regional comparator ≤`$0.001776704`. These are formula evaluations at caps, not forecast charges, actual schema sizes, guarantees of complete output, or totals for the matrix. The larger conservative variant G bound allowing full cache write **in addition** to ordinary input is `$0.00945024` if every input token incurred the listed 0.20 write rate. The intended uncached regional candidate has no explicit write step. Do not apply that write illustration as a documented additive bill.

For Jev, define `S=T_J(serialized state)`, `Q_j=T_J(instructions_j + all option/criterion content_j)`, and unknown wrapper overhead `H_J`:

```
B_J = S + sum_j Q_j + H_J             # Shared-state planning expression
R_J = 0.042 * B_J_bound / 1e6 + verified host extras
Require both billed B_J_bound <=16000 and documented context constraints.
```

Unknown `H_J`/tokenization means no admission yet. If a verified whole billed-input bound were 16,000, the input-only arithmetic would be `$0.000672`; that is **not an established bill for the proposed 98+ questions**. Free output does not remove the need to bound response bytes/latency and validate all answers; Decisions metadata `max_completion_tokens` is not a documented request knob. No reliable failed/aborted-call refund guarantee was established. Hold full reservations for every uncertain attempt.

Liquid documents per-question prompts and aggregate usage in its [models pricing section](https://docs.liquid.ai/lfm/models/decision-models) and [usage definition](https://docs.liquid.ai/lfm/models/d1). Those sources do **not** settle whether all shared text state, criteria and wrappers are billed again for every question. The [maker article](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/d1-decision-model) also claims batching saves input tokens. The implementation/host accounting that reconciles those statements remains unresolved.

A conservative **scenario**, not an actual billed-input inequality or verified lower bound, is:

```
S = bounded shared text-state tokens in the repeated-state scenario
Q_j = bounded instruction/criterion/option tokens for question j
H_j = bounded wrapper/serialization tokens for question j
B_repeat_scenario = sum_j (S + Q_j + H_j)
R_repeat_scenario = 0.02 * B_repeat_scenario / 1e6 + verified host extras
```

Reserve this scenario conservatively only after each unit, wrapper and selected host's accounting are bounded; the expression alone is not a reliable upper bound. Under the hypothetical 98-question/2,500-repeated-state-token assumptions, shared text alone contributes `98 × 2500 = 245000` tokens, before questions/wrappers, so **that scenario** exceeds the approved 16k aggregate classification cap and cannot be admitted. It does not prove every real d1 text encoding is incompatible, nor establish the actual bill. Actual packet sizes, shared-text/criterion treatment and OpenRouter usage/billing mapping remain unresolved; the route stays **not admitted until bounded**, without assuming eligibility either way. Do not reinterpret the approved cap as per-question. Verified current 0.02 input/cached-input pricing and free output remain unchanged; the article's older 0.04 pricing example is historical.
