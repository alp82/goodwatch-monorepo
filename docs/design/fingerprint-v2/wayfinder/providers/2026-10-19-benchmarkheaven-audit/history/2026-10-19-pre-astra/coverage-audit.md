## Economics and transfer to our three jobs

Current maker list rates per million input tokens: **Celeris decision $0.04**, same cached rate, output free; **Kettei fat/chub/core $0.06/$0.04/$0.02**, output free; **Nimble max $0.04**, output free. Nimble explicitly bills shared state/questions once per request despite per-question prompts. Do not transfer that rule to Liquid or Celeris. Kettei account prices/tokenizer/model limits still require bounded inspection downstream. No cache saving is assumed. [Celeris pricing](https://docs.celeris.ai/pricing.md), [Kettei pricing](https://docs.sqwish.ai/guides/pricing.md), [Nimble pricing](https://docs.bespokelabs.ai/nimble/pricing.md)

Sage's public input tiers are $0.05/$0.046/$0.042/$0.038, reasoning $10/M output, search $0.10 each; a free tier exists but current account eligibility is unknown. Its published accounting counts content per question/tag/sort item; batching saves round trips. For those units, render `I = Σ(content + criterion + wrapper)` rather than assuming one shared-state charge. With reasoning/search off, reserve all actual input plus zero-priced response-size bounds; do not use the advertised context as our 16k budget. [Sage pricing](https://levanto.ai/pricing)

For an eligible input-only host, stage cost is `rate × bounded_billed_input / 10^6`; complete cost adds evidence preparation, Qwen companions, repairs, retries and any applicable fixed fees. Local economics require measured device time, memory and operations cost, not “free weights.” Rendered native schemas and billing overhead are missing; no complete matrix total is defensible. Existing $5 shared/$3.50 title ceilings, 16k input, 8192 billed generative output/reasoning, concurrency 2 and 120-second attempts remain planning bounds, not an approved protocol.

The read-only [historical setup audit](/tmp/goodwatch-fingerprint-history-setup/README.md) and [setup deltas](/tmp/goodwatch-fingerprint-history-setup/setup-deltas.md) sharpen admission: Jev1.13 ten-level requests averaged **18,840 input tokens**, already over today's 16k before rich evidence; six-level **13,068 mean is not an upper bound**. Historical roughly $0.79/1,000 Jev was scores-only; roughly $0.85 Qwen included full outputs and substantial cache use. Neither is a current rich complete-pipeline quote. No repeat of the old synopsis-only batch sweep is warranted.

Typed choices can explicitly name unknown/N/A, evidence IDs and positive/negative scoped assertions. Those labels still require a rubric, binding and validation; probability is not factual support. Coarse scale expectations require a declared projection rather than silent rounding. Native highlight/concept/relation representations may change question counts materially. Keep the representation comparison separate from a thin-versus-rich evidence comparison.

For **title knowledge**, none of these sources establishes reliable current film/show knowledge. For **evidence extraction**, native classifiers are plausible on the same checked rich packet, with full prose/advisory/context ownership assigned to companions. For **query interpretation**, routing and relevance capabilities are only hypotheses until our scoped concepts, polarity and relations are checked. General benchmark calibration is not in-domain accuracy; old disagreement with Qwen is not factual error. Rich evidence, changed versions and native schemas are valid changed hypotheses under ADR 0001's revisit condition.

## Proposed resolution delta

Accept this as a coverage addendum to the conditionally ready research decision: the prior shortlist omitted several credible decision fine-tunes/runtimes and hosted alternatives. Consider H2O/Torchcast/Quyet native adapters, Celeris/Kettei host contracts and Decisio shared-state reuse before freezing a challenger. Keep Basal as a resource-dependent lead, Nimble conditional on question count, rerankers as narrow pipeline tools, and the remaining inventory as unverified discovery. **Do not select Jev or any replacement yet.** Representation examples/rubric, bounded rendered requests, permitted existing access and scheduled compatibility are downstream.
