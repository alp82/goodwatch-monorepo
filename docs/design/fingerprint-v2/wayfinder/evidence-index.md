# Fingerprint and discovery evidence

This is the evidence entry point for **Benchmark a richer fingerprint and discovery system**. It preserves the whole investigation: definitions and examples, inventories and ledgers, analysis and untested ideas, implementation constraints, historical experiments, owner decisions and unresolved questions. The [review packet](README.md) contains the exact proposed map and 13 ticket bodies; its [manifest](manifest.json) records their dependencies and publication status.

## How to interpret the evidence

The new definitions, Matrix values and gap recommendations are **proposals or illustrations**, not model results. Catalog tags/tropes are **stored assertions**, not independent reference truth. Synthetic searches demonstrate expressiveness requirements, not demand or measured relevance. Historical runs retain their original dates, configurations, budgets and review limitations. Accepted decisions remain scoped to their experiment and current ADRs; this new map has not selected models, scales or a production design.

A complete [asset catalog](asset-catalog.json) records every local design/audit artifact and its SHA-256. The source manifests retain extraction queries and hashes; the branch ledgers retain inspected examples, residuals and missingness. Existing tracked historical results remain linked in place rather than copied or silently superseded.

The critique behind the design: intensity, centrality, explicitness and quality answer different questions; metadata/production origin is different from aesthetic influence; emotional experience differs from subject matter; viewing suitability depends on audience/context; reputation changes over time. Compound/duplicate fields can overweight similarity. Separate absence from missing evidence, factual advisories from taste preferences, exposure from peak severity, and facts from interpretive judgments. Preserve provenance, edition and movie/show/season/episode scope. Do not infer representation or a creator's identity from aesthetics. Scales and feature selection remain experimental questions.

Top-title selection was vote-count descending, ID ascending, without hidden eligibility filters. Eight movies and eight shows lack tags; five shows lack synopsis. Suspect identities remain visible. These audits do not claim independent viewing of all 3,000 works or semantic review of every tag/trope.

The first scored shortlist is character emphasis, ethical dilemmas, memory, sacrifice and corruption. The trope addendum adds foreshadowing and dramatic irony; symbolism stays optional. Split trust/betrayal/deception deliberately; freedom/mortality already overlap existential meaning; moral decline overlaps fall from grace. Preserve all broader suggestions in branch proposals without turning each into a required dimension.

Other recurring ideas: sparse typed sports/entities/mechanisms/professions/settings/relationships; work/segment/edition color treatment; movie anthologies and richer format conventions; same-scene/same-character/event binding; asserted/averted/subverted/hypothetical modes; peak advisory severity separate from exposure and explicit coverage for exclusions. Parent/child concepts and aliases must not multiply similarity weight. Stored URLs need identity normalization: 1,452 malformed strings affect 3,416 detailed rows. ## Decisions this evidence should inform

Compare title-only knowledge, identity+synopsis extraction, equal controlled rich evidence, identity-masked inputs, and separately costed retrieved evidence. Masking a title does not make a recognizable plot perfectly blind. Include plausible false probes, abstention, polarity, scope, wrong-title aliases and same-participant/event cases. Independently verify reference judgments; do not train and confirm on these openly discussed examples.

Compare binary/category/sparse facts, coarse ordinal strengths, integer 0–10 where useful, counts/durations and explicit unknown/N/A/not-assessed states. Measure inter-reviewer agreement, repeat stability, factuality and judged retrieval rather than Qwen correlation alone. Keep confidence separate from attribute strength.

Evaluate essence_text, essence_tags, stable valid highlight_keys and every companion output through an output-by-consumer matrix. Compare one full generator, classifier plus prose/evidence pipeline, reuse/derivation and selective escalation. Include evidence acquisition, retries, prose, embeddings/index updates and cached/uncached queries in equivalent-output cost/latency. Numeric maxima do not automatically make useful defining highlights.

Use staged screening and reduced experiment combinations to control cost/time. Freeze a new holdout only after protocol/human review. No new paid experiment, schema migration, production backfill or provider switch was executed or authorized by preparing this packet.
