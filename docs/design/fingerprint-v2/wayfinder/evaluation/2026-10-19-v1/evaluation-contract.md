## 1. Purpose and agreed priorities

Choose the least expensive complete pipeline that delivers useful, faithful title descriptions and discovery, at acceptable speed. First establish whether failures arise from missing title knowledge, unsupported extraction, representation, query interpretation, or ranking. A faster classifier cannot supply facts absent from its evidence; a richer vector alone cannot repair missing prose or an incorrect exclusion. [E2–E5]

Agreed quality-first order for the next comparison:

1. Faithful title analysis: core experiential traits plus supported precise concepts, with essence text/tags, representative valid highlights, advisories and other required companions assessed separately.
2. Discovery usefulness: descriptive and compound searches, negation, related-title contrasts and Mood/Theme/Style membership checks. Use these as offline cases, not new UI work. Exact-title/person identity and catalog predicates remain guardrails.
3. Complete-output cost and bulk throughput, then interactive query latency. Prefer a cheaper/faster candidate when judged quality is indistinguishable; expose the tradeoff rather than collapsing all metrics into a single score.

Taste match recalibration, Explorer layouts, personal order, real-user watch outcomes and production migration remain later acceptance work. No first-pilot claim of recommendation lift or universal catalog quality; the familiar 1990–2010 cohort deliberately narrows the initial review. The 547-attribute proposal is neither the definition of success nor a mandatory output. [E1–E3]

## 2. Vocabulary and quality policy

Existing glossary: **Title analysis** is the complete generated document; **Fingerprint** currently means its 74 integer scores; a **Fingerprint verdict** is an explained reviewer judgment, not agreement with Qwen. Keep those meanings. Use “candidate V2 representation” for experimental outputs. These evaluation terms clarify the experiment; they do not revise CONTEXT.md or the production domain model.

- **Supported absence:** evidence explicitly supports absence within a stated work/season/episode/edition and coverage. Different from **unknown** (insufficient/conflicting evidence), **not assessed** (outside this run), and **not applicable** (rubric does not apply). Zero is assessed absence/lowest strength only where the rubric defines it. Null needs a reason; omitted fields cannot silently become zero.
- **Hard exclusion:** a condition the result must satisfy, unlike a soft preference. Strict “no X” results require supported absence with adequate coverage. Unknown cases do not pass that filter; return fewer/no verified matches and report withheld coverage. A low score, missing trope, or missing tag is not proof of absence. Test false assurances separately from ordinary ranking errors.
- **Scoped assertion:** a claim identifies its title and scope, polarity and supporting evidence. Work-wide co-occurrence does not prove a same-scene chase or same-character relationship. An averted trope is not an asserted event; a metaphorical chess match is not depicted chess. Do not invent timestamps, edition facts or identity claims.

Agreed advancement policy: preserve failures as evidence, but do not advance an arm with a confirmed wrong-title association, false hard-exclusion assurance, fabricated decisive evidence or invalid required companion output until fixed and re-evaluated. One confirmed critical failure pauses that arm for diagnosis; it does not invalidate the entire research program. No semantic retry to hide the failure.

Prefer honest unknowns over invented facts, while reporting coverage so abstaining on everything cannot win. Increased unknown rates or weaker soft-trait/highlight/relevance results may be explored on development data; they do not qualify as an improvement automatically. Human review must accept any material tradeoff before finalist selection. Numeric non-inferiority margins and production latency/throughput targets remain unset for diagnostic screening, then must be agreed before fresh confirmation. The quality-first answer adopts this diagnostic, profiling-only phase; it does not approve numerical promotion thresholds or waive later confirmation. [E2–E4, E8]

## 3. References, disagreement and review effort

Build reference packets from existing attributable evidence with identity, scope, date, coverage and source reliability. Generated essence/tropes and historical Qwen values are candidate assertions, not reference truth. Agents locate factual support; ask the human about goals or interpretive preferences, not discoverable plot facts. Mark unverifiable claims unknown rather than requiring the human to remember them.

Agreed allowance: **45 minutes total human review across the initial development round**, aiming for **20 paired reference/relevance judgments and six complete-output spot checks**, with critical disagreements first. These are review targets inside a time cap, not a promise that every target fits. If it expires, preserve unreviewed status and pause advancement. Astra assessment preparation is separate from the human's 45 minutes; record agent review effort too.

**A frontier-model quality assessment is required for every review round, before the human makes picks.** Use existing authorized **Astra agent sessions**, not separately billed judge APIs, so this does not consume or enlarge the $5 provider-call envelope. Record actual model/session, rubric, evidence, output coverage and limitations. If an authorized Astra session is unavailable, pause the review-dependent pick; do not silently waive the assessment or substitute a paid API. The earlier historical Astra/Fable waiver remains a historical fact, but does not override this new requirement. [E6]

Default sequence: shuffle candidate labels and conceal provider identities/rank/cost during Astra's initial quality assessment where possible. Astra judges the actual candidate outputs against attributable evidence, recording supported claims, errors, unknowns, scope/coverage, companion completeness and uncertainty. Preserve its initial judgment before incorporating human feedback; disclose prior exposure if that session has seen candidates, outcomes or historical preferences. Candidate labels alone cannot establish judge independence.

Then show the human a concise evidence-grounded summary and **recommendations for their picks**, including quality/cost/speed tradeoffs once measurements exist. Do not withhold the requested recommendation for experimental purity. Capture the exact summary and recommendations shown, candidate-label key/access, relevant exposure and subsequent human choices. A human shown Astra's recommendation supplies **assisted judgments**, even if candidate providers remain blinded. Analyze those separately from any genuinely independent judgments; agreement with Astra is descriptive post-exposure agreement, not independent inter-reviewer validation or a truth signal. No independent human-review requirement is imposed. Neither frontier judgments, historical Qwen agreement nor personal ratings are gold.

Use fact-supported/contradicted/unknown for factual claims; ordinal relevance 0–3 for discovery (0 contradicts or is irrelevant; 1 partial; 2 useful; 3 strongly fits). No automatic averaging of disputed truth. Correct demonstrably wrong references with an audit note and rescore all arms; unresolved interpretive cases retain both views and sensitivity results. If they change the winner, the human decides the preference or confirmation remains inconclusive.

## 4. Measurements by stage

All results retain attempted/planned/valid/reviewed counts, first-attempt and repaired outcomes, cache state, configuration, raw output and missingness. Small-sample percentiles are descriptive, with n and maximum; per-request medians are not throughput forecasts.

| Stage | Quality | Cost, latency and throughput |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence preparation/acquisition | Correct identity, provenance, source/edition/season coverage; independent support versus generated claims | Per-title extraction/enrichment cost; shared evidence costs allocated to every deployment design needing it; wall/CPU time and human minutes. Stored local material only in this envelope. |
| Title analysis, including prose/companions | Structural validity separately from supported claims, contradiction/unknown rates, trait rubric agreement, highlights, spoilers, advisories, repeat drift and completeness | USD per attempted, valid and human-accepted complete title; separate cold/warm prompt cache, retries, reasoning, prose and escalation; p50/p95 request and complete-title time; accepted titles/hour at measured concurrency. |
| Embedding/index preparation | Output dimensions/coverage, feature order/version, input identity and controlled retrieval parity | Numeric vector derivation separately from text encoding; CPU-seconds, RAM peak, bytes/title, build/update time, titles/second; paid embedding fees if used. Local compute is not economically free. Report resource units if its dollar allocation is unknown. |
| Query interpretation | Intent, entity, polarity, hard/soft constraint and binding correctness; abstention/coverage, repeat stability | USD/1,000 fresh readings and cached lookups separately; requests/query; p50/p95 interpretation and queue wait; successful readings/second. No invented cache-hit rate. |
| Retrieval/ranking only | Fully graded pooled top-10 nDCG, useful results, wrong top-5 and exclusion violations; exact-title guardrail; recall only over established eligible references | Fixed cached readings and candidate corpus; p50/p95 encoding, retrieval, ranking; CPU/RAM and completed queries/second at stated load. No “free search” claim just because ranking adds no API call. |
| End-to-end discovery | Useful top results plus correctly applied catalog/hard constraints; coverage and failure/partial-result rates | Fresh/cached search p50/p95; first usable delivery separately from completed answer; queue/timeout/busy rates, successful QPS; total cost combining amortized title preparation, index work and query stages. |

Keep stage timings inclusive/exclusive and parallel overlap explicit; sums of stage p95s are not end-to-end p95. Report hard-filter precision alongside withheld-result counts. Grade all pooled results or mark that comparison incomplete. Cost per 1,000 titles is not cost per 1,000 searches. Give amortization scenarios in terms of reuse/query volume rather than assuming demand. Match machines, cache state, corpus and output coverage across comparisons. [E4, E7–E10]

## 5. Development first, fresh confirmation later

The historical ten titles, arena splits (including holdout5), and 256 + 78 discussed scenarios are development evidence, not an automatically selected review cohort. The evidence index incorrectly calls holdout5 “five-query”; its companion index and original contract specify approximately 30 queries. Do not inherit that summary typo. [E1, E7]

Agreed small development capacity: **at most three complete title-pipeline configurations, each with 12 output slots (10 distinct titles + two unchanged-input repeats)**. For the initial human review, select **familiar titles from the user’s own recorded ratings, released in 1990–2010 inclusive, mostly highly rated with a few low-rated contrasts**. Personal ratings guide familiarity/preferences, never factual accuracy. Sol is locating the rating evidence read-only; this work does not duplicate that lookup. Exact identities, high/low rating definitions, movie/show mix and title/year handling belong to [Choose the title corpus, evidence arms, and reference judgments](https://github.com/alp82/goodwatch-monorepo/issues/720). A reversible suggestion is eight high-rated and two low-rated titles if ten suitable familiar titles are supported; this is not a settled mix or authority to fill gaps with invented ratings. Use negative/scope/identity probes within supported familiar cases where feasible. This cohort cannot establish whole-catalog, recent/new, obscure-title or unfamiliar-work quality; those remain later coverage needs. Query capacity, when its map prerequisites are satisfied: **two configurations × 12 query slots (10 distinct + two repeats)**. These are ceilings, not a requirement to spend every slot. Exact cohort, scales, companion contract and arm allocation remain with their existing decision tickets.

Use matched inputs for extraction comparisons. Separate title-only knowledge, synopsis extraction, controlled rich evidence and identity-masked probes where a focused question requires them; masking a recognizable plot is imperfect blinding. Do not multiply all models × scales × evidence modes. Choose a bounded contrast; if it cannot fit, narrow scope or seek a new envelope. Retrieved/enriched evidence is separately costed, and common enrichment must not conceal dependence on another generator.

Fresh confirmation is **not funded or authorized by this development approval**. Familiarity and prior personal ratings alone do not make a case fresh; exposure to experimental cases, outputs and tuning governs that designation. Freeze finalist configurations, criteria and independently prepared reference judgments before exposure to fresh title/query cases; split by underlying work/franchise and near-duplicate query intent. Separate custodian access from tuning where possible, record exposure, score once, and report uncertainty by title/query rather than treating hundreds of correlated traits as independent samples. Contamination turns that set into development data; any retuned winner needs another fresh set. Confirmation sample size depends on observed variance and review capacity, not an invented universal n.

## 6. Approved development envelope; execution still gated

**Approved: USD 5 maximum incremental provider-call spend across this first development round, shared across stages and all participating tickets, with no automatic replenishment.** This is the human’s new Q3 approval, not an inherited budget. The program has no approval beyond this envelope; confirmation and later rounds return for approval. **No spending yet:** lineup, corpus/evidence, representation, output contract and executable protocol dependencies remain. Query allocations remain dormant until their prototype dependencies are satisfied. No runner receives a separate $5 allowance.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Shared evidence enrichment from local inputs | 0.50 | 12 | 3 | 15 |

The title allocation permits at most two initial generation stages per complete-output slot; query allocation permits two reading calls per query slot. More components mean fewer slots, not a cap bypass. Embedding batches contain at most 36 inputs each. Provider subcalls, repairs, timeouts and discovered retries count; unused stages do not transfer their budgets automatically. A later external evidence-fetching plan needs its own permission and limits; no crawling is included.

Agreed controls, unchanged from the presented envelope:

- Global paid-call concurrency **2**, at most **one logical title/query pipeline at once**; its two independent subcalls may overlap. No provider fanout test beyond this. Local retrieval load probes: concurrency **1 then 2**, at most **200 completed attempts total**, on an isolated local setup with a **30-minute wall-clock cap**. No production load tests or provisioning.
- Per logical stage/input: **one retry maximum**, within the shared stage retry count; disable hidden SDK/job retries. Retry transient failures or mechanically constrained structure repairs only, with unchanged source evidence. Record original output; factual disagreement and abstention get no quality-improvement retry.
- Generative request ceilings: **16,000 input tokens and 8,192 total billed completion/reasoning tokens**, including repair context. Classification/batched-question requests require a verified billable-token bound too; the same input ceiling applies unless the human changes it. **120-second attempt timeout; 60-second maximum retry wait**, otherwise stop that route. These are new resource limits, not imported model-quality requirements.
- Reserve worst-case input/output/cache/request charges before dispatch against both stage and total limits. Unreconciled calls retain their full reservation. Keep observed charges, rate estimates and held reservations separate. Do not launch if no reliable cost bound exists; no purchase, new account policy or shared-key change is permitted. A provider spending cap is useful only if already available and isolated.
- Stop the affected route on auth/privacy/schema-cap incompatibility, unexpected model/provider, two successive transient failures, or unexplained costs. Stop all paid dispatch if accounting fails or a reservation would exceed any cap. Preserve not-attempted denominators. No automatic replacement provider, added arm or budget transfer.
- Review after the first **three title slots per configuration**, inside the ceiling. Stop ineffective arms early; resume only when references/contract issues are resolved. Repeated output-schema failure or confirmed critical factual failure pauses the arm. End at the request/spend/review ceiling, even if inconclusive.

**Only the $5 option was selected.** The $0 and $2 alternatives are superseded. The higher ceiling accommodates more expensive complete outputs or richer inputs; it adds no samples, calls, retries, concurrency or confirmation. Current route/pricing research must establish feasibility; unfit arms stop rather than silently spending more. The $0.40 contingency is held for unresolved charges and cannot fund optional work; retain each uncertain call's full reservation without counting the same exposure twice. No automatic transfers between stage ceilings.

Historical rationale: 101 attempts in the earlier broad comparison cost **$0.3764186484**, with **$0.3525869667** retained reservations (combined **$0.7290056151**, not $0.729 of measured charges). At observed historical per-output averages, 36 Qwen3.8 outputs would be about **$0.03047**, while 36 Grok outputs would be **$1.03012**; at three times the latter historical cost, about **$3.09**. The approved $5 therefore buys cost headroom within the unchanged small-screen limits. None of these extrapolations prices V2, cold prompts or rich inputs. Historical Qwen prompt caching was substantial. Jev's ~$0.17 sweep and ~2,000 calls are not permission to repeat that fanout. [E4–E6]
