## Advisory topics

- **violence**: Physical violence or intentional bodily harm.
- **gore**: Graphic wounds, blood, or bodily destruction.
- **sexual_activity**: Depicted or discussed sexual activity.
- **nudity**: Depicted nudity, independently of sexual activity.
- **substance_use**: Use of intoxicating or addictive substances.
- **profanity**: Strong or profane language.
- **disturbing_imagery**: Imagery likely to be upsetting through its content rather than plot stakes alone.
- **child_endangerment**: Threatened or actual harm to a child.
- **animal_harm**: Threatened or actual injury, cruelty, or death affecting an animal.
- **eating_disorders**: Eating-disorder behavior or discussion.
- **self_harm**: Intentional self-injury, separately from suicidal behavior.
- **suicide**: Suicidal ideation, attempts, or death by suicide.
- **sexual_violence**: Sexual assault, coercive sexual conduct, or threats of such conduct.
- **domestic_abuse**: Abuse within intimate or domestic relationships.
- **police_brutality**: Abusive force or treatment by police.
- **torture**: Deliberate infliction of severe suffering on a captive or controlled person.
- **kidnapping_and_confinement**: Abduction or involuntary confinement.
