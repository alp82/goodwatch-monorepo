# Verdict: BLOCK

This is a static read of the inline bytes only. Tools were disabled, so I recomputed no hash or byte count, ran no test, and make no execution, capacity, latency or production claim.

Both blockers fail closed. Neither can produce a false `completed`, but B1 makes the run unable to finish after the one-shot adoption is spent, and B2 destroys the original refusal evidence.

## Blockers

### B1. `executor_v2.context()` refuses the full stage for every operation that loads it

- **Where:** `external/executor_v2.py`, `context()`, the final `if canonical(config)+b'\n'!=control_raw: raise RuntimeError('executor_policy_changed')`.
- **Cause:** for `path==adoption.STAGE`, `control_raw` is the frozen `prepared-control-policy-cpu0.json` (3c99f666…, 2289 B), which is indented multi-line JSON. Its compact canonical form can never equal those bytes. Only the admission derivative is canonical.
- **Affected operations:** `turnover`, `outer-admit`, `register-consumer`, and `cleanup` once the stage exists.
- **Consequence:** `adopt-running-clone`, `preflight` and `stage` succeed. The intent, the v2 admission scope, the adoption handoff and the full stage are all spent; then `turnover` refuses permanently. `cleanup` also loses its recipe and controller, so no handoff is committed and cleanup is reported unknown.
- **Minimal correction:** apply the canonical-bytes check only when `path==adoption.ADMISSION`. The preceding `control_raw!=expected` already binds the stage bytes exactly. No guard is lowered.
- **Why tests miss it:** `test_executor_v2.py` builds a canonical synthetic policy, and `test_executor_v2_close.py` / `test_executor_v2_cleanup.py` mock `context`.

### B2. Consumer preflight masks any refusal after enrolment with a `NameError`

- **Where:** `backend14/cpu0_preflight.py`, `_preflight()`, the `except BaseException as error:` block: `'cleanupAddressableByFixedReceipt':issued`.
- **Cause:** `issued` is a local of `_enrol_consumer` and is undefined in `_preflight`.
- **Trigger:** any failure after the preliminary receipt is published — the entry sample (`host_emergency_floor`, `swap_present`), `lifecycle_connection_present`, a re-fence drift, or a handoff commit failure.
- **Consequence:** `main()` emits `guard_or_runtime_failure` with no `admissionFailureEvidence` and no `consumerEnrolmentFailureEvidence`. The original reason and the same-read numeric sample are lost at the most likely refusal point.
- **Minimal correction:** replace `issued` with `True`, as the `finally` branch already does.
- **Why tests miss it:** the `enrolled-failure` case in `test_capacity_cpu0.mjs` uses a synthetic Python stand-in, and `test_cpu0_controller.py` exercises `_preflight` only without `consumer_context`.
